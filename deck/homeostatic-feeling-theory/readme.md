# Homeostatic Feeling Theory of Consciousness

Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted, unreviewed. Not written or endorsed by Antonio Damasio and Hanna Damasio. To propose a correction, open an issue or pull request that changes the record and cites the source.

This is a [form.surf](https://form.surf) model repository for the account of consciousness developed by Antonio Damasio, much of it with Hanna Damasio: consciousness begins with homeostatic feelings, the spontaneously conscious experience of the state of the body's interior, such as hunger, thirst, pain and well-being. Each claim is one JSON record in `proposition/`, with its kind (definitional, empirical, philosophical), its epistemic status, and the source and locator it was taken from. Claims about interoceptive physiology, the brainstem and machine learners are empirical. The identification of feeling with the start of consciousness and of subjectivity is philosophical. Findings from a lesion patient and from homeostatic neural networks are in `observation/`, and `link/` joins claims to each other and to evidence. Views the authors argue against, and objections they answer, are kept as claims with `voice: "critique"`. Claims stated only in the books or in papers with no open copy are left out.

Every record has `provenance.ai_written: true` and `reviewed: false` until a named person checks it against its sources.

Format: form.surf/1. Check with `form check .`.
