# Homeostatic Feeling Theory of Consciousness

The account starts from life regulation. [Homeostasis](record:23f09c18-8151-4d2b-b696-a6d8d73ba148) is the regulation of internal states within a range compatible with life, and [sensing, feeling and consciousness all serve it](record:faf66f39-9e71-41c6-87be-0d63adf3081f). [Sensing is everywhere in nature but covert](record:b5192ab7-c35b-4205-bbd5-306cb2f6de78): bacteria and plants respond intelligently without knowing they have sensed anything, because [without a nervous system they cannot map or picture their own state](record:bd437704-6498-45e5-bd8f-ccebb8dd6605). [Nervous systems open the way to overt knowledge](record:fbf3f6d0-2369-4d39-8767-48a689208957), beginning with knowledge of the organism itself.

That first knowledge comes as [feelings, conscious experiences of the body's internal state](record:60daece9-49cc-4ef0-9f92-5b5ca4d6a727), and the basic class is [homeostatic feelings](record:91bcf6fd-0808-4818-a6b4-ad2e676378a7), which arise spontaneously with deviations from physiological balance. [Feelings of emotion are patterned combinations of homeostatic shifts](record:93bf78ce-31c8-4821-9df0-14fce80a0e16). The central claim is that [homeostatic feelings are the inaugural events of consciousness](record:9a3aecf3-7b12-46fe-a302-20d99f34cc64): each one informs the organism that it exists and how it is faring, and [it can guide action only because it is spontaneously known](record:7f24cb8e-e135-4be9-bc89-336395d15a54). [Consciousness is defined low in biology](record:d6358680-ac7b-4f65-bdb1-256fa424e68d), as the means for an organism to experience the state of operations inside its body.

The feelings come from a particular physiology. [Interoceptive axons are unmyelinated or lightly myelinated](record:95b17338-6785-4a4a-8a4c-2f98c660201b), and [parts of the interoceptive system lie outside the blood-brain barrier](record:39c3368e-d593-4ba9-8c32-08bde3e8e9ae), so [neural and non-neural operations comingle](record:2af4d7a7-23d6-4b80-aeb2-4958c81e2e32). [Interoception is analog where the rest of the mind is digital](record:931503c6-8254-4f1c-b87c-bb66db950a28), and it uses [non-synaptic signaling](record:19c77a6f-8e88-4588-a3b2-3135851beb6d). [The vagus nerve maps the interior](record:d81d5e6c-62b5-4255-a75c-ff74ffa9863c) onto the [nucleus tractus solitarius, the first hub of self-representation](record:4bf329d1-3737-4c2e-86ce-1c921f201c8b), so [consciousness is rooted in the brainstem rather than the cortex](record:5a91500b-7405-4c75-8730-cbd18924aab2). [Consciousness arises with the nervous system but not from it alone](record:07808012-1a8e-475e-b656-2731f32276fa).

The self follows. [Feelings joined to images of the surround give a subjective perspective](record:88046b31-23d9-44f8-8699-bd612948d327), the likely origin of the self. The mind divides into [a feeling mind and a modern mind](record:ba84d7c1-a1bb-45e9-9b41-890651ba31ff), and [consciousness proper combines the two](record:263fc1c5-3a3f-45ec-8dd6-79b659e1928b). A lesion patient whose [self-awareness survived bilateral destruction of the insula, cingulate and medial prefrontal cortex](record:ecf905f9-6688-491e-9f1e-4f80228ec869) fits [the brainstem account of primordial feelings](record:cfc091e3-ebd5-4eb3-844b-0ddeccd5a690), against the [insula-centered view](record:74b5a510-4c25-40f1-9332-ba44a1369fd4). On machines, [current AI lacks interoceptive processing](record:e7141987-848a-426d-a005-eb340f20b377), and Man, Damasio and Neven built [learners made vulnerable to what they compute over](record:aade0c29-4405-4fd2-af90-6ac1e687d072), which [adapt better under concept shift](record:787dfee7-b450-4e5a-84eb-561d599bf130).
