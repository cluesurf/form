# Panpsychism

Panpsychism is the view that [consciousness is a fundamental and ubiquitous feature of the natural world](record:b7030a85-6d84-4e67-a95a-17f915f6f1b7). Its contemporary defenders mean [experience, not thought](record:5754d8b7-db57-4627-818b-ecf2044fe810): an electron, on the view, has something it is like to be it, of an extremely simple kind, and no hopes or beliefs.

Two arguments carry most of the weight. The first is Galen Strawson's. A [realistic physicalist](record:407f6095-8f02-4de9-b7c3-cdfc8f0e7d6d) must grant that [experience is real](record:61b66a5f-60b8-4624-b9be-c39317574e14) and that it is physical. Most physicalists also hold that the physical in itself is wholly non-experiential, which forces them to say that experience emerges from the wholly non-experiential. Strawson argues that [emergence cannot be brute](record:dcfa574a-8d24-49bf-aff7-2791cd03e0b5): whatever emerges must emerge in virtue of something in what it emerges from. [Liquidity reduces without remainder to its molecules](record:11aaf766-cd93-44db-98bd-34665df3693f), experience does not, so [experience cannot emerge from the wholly non-experiential](record:694b6540-89e4-4be9-bafd-83d75b83211d). At least some ultimates must be experiential, and Strawson takes [micropsychism to be panpsychism](record:fe93a968-2b12-45bf-9968-36be9a82d7de), so [real physicalism entails panpsychism](record:12f2f28c-801d-4939-aa5d-2e21f18bd622).

The second is the Russellian, or intrinsic nature, argument. [Physics describes only the structure and dispositions of matter](record:e921cede-0144-43d4-8653-fc661e6ebe4b), what matter does and not what it is. A world of pure dispositions [passes the buck forever](record:320c5da8-6d48-4ffb-804e-e43d949b3200), so matter needs a categorical nature, and [consciousness is offered as that nature](record:d4dfed29-1268-46b9-9759-f16f6ea008b5). The only categorical nature known from the inside is the consciousness of brains, and Goff argues that [the simplest hypothesis extends it to the rest of matter](record:628beb86-e300-4b42-84a9-13362b9ebc5b). On this reading [panpsychism is a research program](record:93006735-e619-47a3-8214-a7e29df4d0d1) that explains complex consciousness by simpler consciousness.

The view comes in kinds. [Constitutive panpsychism](record:3ec31ce1-6ec9-431f-81ef-ddc7e6970b47) grounds human consciousness in more basic consciousness, which promises [to keep consciousness causally efficacious](record:48c951f9-1b2b-478c-9760-0e05a2199768), and in Chalmers's analysis [only its Russellian form](record:e8577801-4971-4afe-a3fe-9c7bee667920) escapes both the arguments against physicalism and those against dualism. [Emergentist panpsychism](record:16e891c1-4aeb-405e-ae28-19ec2aaf16e5) has human minds brought into being by micro-level consciousness. [Micropsychism](record:61f67d34-01be-4761-bcf8-40d747fe3838) grounds everything at the micro-level, [cosmopsychism](record:f9c93a59-1bb6-403e-9173-efd7bdf7916f) in a conscious universe.

The main open question is [the combination problem](record:148f0391-5053-4f26-a7d3-a936561719d9): how little subjects make a big one. Goff's own [no summing of subjects](record:c280d1b4-9a23-43b3-8365-b97fdd78ecd2) principle and [microexperiential zombies](record:5944b389-ed55-4ddb-9762-3fa8ae42e576) press it, Coleman argues that [points of view cannot combine](record:c8e2e4ef-360f-4ff5-84af-5b6e11836dda), and Chalmers adds [the palette problem](record:f36c0125-e12d-4e33-814b-32337a20fb57) and [structural mismatch](record:c21d2b88-da5a-4b74-b721-72470765eebf). Goff answers with [phenomenal bonding](record:76ec8d6d-4df8-42b2-96d5-0e67ed39b7cf), a relation between subjects that [no transparent concept captures](record:eafc1b26-9f68-4b77-84ca-a842927c561f), and later argues that [micropsychism needs a deflationary view of human experience](record:8d027513-0aff-44ef-9d53-e7da6867e010) and that [cosmopsychism is to be preferred](record:4891f2b0-3c96-4488-9bc0-cf0c6ed4a2b8), at the price of a [de-combination problem](record:14d45f5e-4563-4fe1-91f3-5d4ab2142be3). His [hybrid cosmopsychism](record:a5694dcb-9cf6-4e09-be6b-0aa1e00f3ab3) has new subjects emerge while [inheriting experience the universe already had](record:012fbe82-3425-458f-9b35-9f89604c7bdd), so that [physics runs the same with or without them](record:d60ce877-893d-4a1c-9861-def798bfce09). Strawson concedes the framework [offers nothing to scientific test](record:37e99694-1355-41bf-b80f-28af31d04d95), and the field's own consensus is that [no solution to the combination problem is yet adequate](record:41ca5304-9664-41ae-a344-b23d5f38385f).
