# form

The records behind [form.surf](https://form.surf): theories of consciousness
and reality, each stated as structured claims, evidence and links, and
the patterns between them: what two or more have in common or at issue.

Nobody competes here. Each folder states one framework's own claims from
its public sources, with its own verdict on each. The patterns folder
points at those claims: the same idea in several frameworks, each in its
own words, and every contradiction and tension between them, kept
visible. Standing comes from evidence or derivation, never a vote.

## Status

Every record outside `vibe/` is community-written, drafted by AI from the
framework's published sources, and unreviewed unless its
`provenance.reviewed` is true. No framework's authors wrote or endorsed
their folder. Corrections are welcome as pull requests, with the source
and locator that shows the error.

A framework's author is welcome to check their folder and reply in their
own words. A way for authors to look after their own folder is planned,
never with a veto over the claims.

## Format

Each folder is one repository in format `form.surf/1`: a `form.json`, one
JSON file per record in `proposition/`, `observation/`, `procedure/`,
`link/`, `source/` and `question/`, prose in `paper/`, and an `AGENTS.md`
for any AI agent working in it. Every folder must pass `form check` before
a commit.
