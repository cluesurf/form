# Quantum panprotopsychism (Gambini and Pullin)

Gambini and Pullin start from what physics can say. Its laws describe regularities in how things behave and say nothing about what things are in themselves, a position they call [regularism](record:5f5b559b-e83d-4dce-ada4-f178e1d0fabc). Classical physics is causally closed, so any inner aspect of matter would do nothing. Quantum mechanics is not: [its probabilistic laws leave room](record:381c8ebe-c7b5-493d-9430-dcab5ccba03b) for something that is not fixed by the laws without breaking them.

They take the quantum axioms as an ontology: [systems in states, which are dispositions to produce events in other systems](record:516fac38-6c55-470f-b7c2-375ba98b191e). Events are the actual entities, and states [have the same objective status](record:3b991ce0-6f04-470c-8e2d-cba7b6d90496). To this ontology they add an internal phenomenal side, [protophenomenal regularism](record:09af6c8e-3b02-41c8-b72e-35e081303ee3), close to the event ontology Russell proposed. The combination problem asks how tiny protophenomenal properties sum to a mind. Its forms [assume that a whole supervenes on its parts](record:7645b291-acbb-4bb4-820a-da21c254ee0b), which fails for entangled systems, where [the state space grows exponentially](record:c831600e-bc9d-4c67-88ac-8eff8795f1e3) and the parts have no properties of their own. That [answers the palette problem](record:f0082b2d-aa45-49c7-a333-6184e52b6ca2).

The second paper finds the subject. Using Whitehead's prehension, [a subject is the phenomenal side of a system in a state](record:7c0bfe97-5d1f-4e95-a4b9-b753932db99f), living from the event that prepares it to the event it produces. [Preparation is a proto-perception and measurement a proto-volition](record:dec3ecad-d3f6-43aa-a472-d8519ef18389). In an entangled system [only the whole is a subject](record:da6c2cb9-0e5e-4157-ba3c-53d90bb1662b), which answers James's subject-summing problem, and because prehensions take time, [the felt now has thickness](record:2c4dbf9e-7aa9-4db7-97a2-74967c7316a5).

The third paper adds [self-consciousness as viewing oneself as a subject](record:4e593cee-9b7f-428f-b51d-b5c64b2ac325) and [choice made possible by causal openness](record:586212b8-2682-419b-a329-ff88ee4e721a). [Personal identity rests on quantum states that cannot be cloned](record:468d8fca-3986-48bc-b6ec-1ade6000bc21), which removes the teleportation and fission paradoxes. All of this [requires an entangled quantum system coupled to the brain's neurons](record:09af802a-e1bf-4406-8130-4182d2738cf0), against [the objection that the brain decoheres too fast](record:7b883220-aac1-4717-8ac1-3e9cfa2f4249), and the authors point to Fisher's Posner molecules. The fourth paper concludes that [the universe is consciousness-centered](record:2578af97-8db3-469e-8c3b-0591c04f0ed4), against the nihilism they trace to classical mechanism.
