# Quantum Panprotopsychism

Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted, unreviewed. Not written or endorsed by Rodolfo Gambini and Jorge Pullin. To propose a correction, open an issue or pull request that changes the record and cites the source.

This is a [form.surf](https://form.surf) model repository for the quantum panprotopsychism of the physicists Rodolfo Gambini and Jorge Pullin: the axioms of quantum mechanics describe systems in states that produce events in other systems, those states and events have an internal phenomenal aspect, and entangled systems have properties their parts lack, so the classical assumptions behind the combination problem fail. Each claim is one JSON record in `proposition/`, with its kind (definitional, empirical, philosophical, normative), its epistemic status, and the source and page it was taken from. Most claims are philosophical. The requirement that the brain hold an entangled quantum system coupled to its neurons is empirical and untested. The fourth paper, by Gambini alone, draws the cosmological and humanistic conclusions, and its claims are marked hypothesized. Objections the papers answer are kept as claims with `voice: "critique"`.

Every record has `provenance.ai_written: true` and `reviewed: false` until a named person checks it against its sources.

Format: form.surf/1. Check with `form check .`.
