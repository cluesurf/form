# Felt Uncertainty Theory

Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted, unreviewed. Not written or endorsed by Mark Solms, Karl Friston and Jaak Panksepp. To propose a correction, open an issue or pull request that changes the record and cites the source.

This is a [form.surf](https://form.surf) model repository for felt uncertainty theory, the account of consciousness Mark Solms set out in "The Conscious Id" (2013), with Jaak Panksepp in 2012, with Karl Friston in 2018, in "The Hard Problem of Consciousness and the Free Energy Principle" (2019) and in the book The Hidden Spring (2021). Its claims: consciousness is generated in the upper brainstem, not the cortex, its elemental form is affect, affect is an extended form of homeostasis, and formally it is the precision a self-organizing system assigns to its own homeostatic prediction errors, felt as pleasure when uncertainty falls and unpleasure when it rises. Panksepp's evidence for seven primary-process emotional systems shared by all mammals, and Bjorn Merker's observations of children born without a cortex, are its main supporting sources. Each claim is one JSON record in `proposition/`, with its kind (definitional, empirical, mathematical, philosophical), its epistemic status, and the source and page or section it was taken from. Findings the sources report, such as decorticate animals and hydranencephalic children, are in `observation/`. Published objections, from Susanne Watkins and Geraint Rees on the hydranencephaly evidence and from Adam Safron on the sufficiency of the brainstem, are kept as claims with `voice: "critique"`. Related folders: `homeostatic-feeling-theory` holds Antonio Damasio's account, which shares the brainstem and homeostasis but has no free energy formalism and ties feeling to nervous systems mapping the body. `free-energy-principle` holds Friston's general principle, which this theory applies to feeling. `integrated-world-modeling-theory` holds Safron's theory, which accepts the role of affect but argues the brainstem alone is not sufficient for experience.

Every record has `provenance.ai_written: true` and `reviewed: false` until a named person checks it against its sources.

Format: form.surf/1. Check with `form check .`.
