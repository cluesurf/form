# Felt Uncertainty Theory

Most research on the neural correlates of consciousness takes vision as its model and the cerebral cortex as its seat. Solms argues that this was [looking in the wrong place](record:0a0168a5-040d-4c13-aa35-6ed32350c3aa). Animals with the cortex removed and children born without one remain awake, emotional and responsive, while small lesions in the upper brainstem abolish consciousness. By the rule that a function localized in a structure is lost with that structure, [consciousness is generated in the upper brainstem](record:262805f6-ee6c-45ea-9518-fe92e9023a04), and the cortex, which [is unconscious in itself](record:c1d56873-98b8-4225-a4f6-7c324e3641f9), lends consciousness to perception and thought only when [feeling is extended onto it](record:640399ac-d548-4b96-aff3-cf32927cfb49).

What the brainstem generates is [affect](record:c9fd3e4a-360c-49c0-b769-d25d4177bde7), so [consciousness is fundamentally affective](record:3a96c5a0-1bd3-4987-aa15-bd9cf150da5d) and [the experiencing subject is constituted by affect](record:d0f26a31-e808-4668-985e-870534314bf6). Asking why vision does not go on in the dark is reasonable, Solms says, but [a feeling that is not felt makes no sense](record:f48bf39f-81e6-4ac1-8cb3-56b9e1f253eb), which is why the hard problem should be posed about feeling. With Panksepp, the [three levels of consciousness](record:02f6663d-6998-4b3d-a8af-7212ae480564), affective, perceptual and cognitive, map onto the brain's evolutionary layers, and [the id is the source of consciousness while the ego is unconscious](record:a135dcc8-311e-4804-ae24-4d21331b8cf5).

The view rests on [dual-aspect monism](record:94919012-e63a-4afa-b389-0fe45c89f135): the brain [does not produce consciousness](record:2fc5cb7b-6023-4fd9-89b2-8cc7b2f07b9d), body and mind are two appearances of one function, and that function should be stated in abstract, ideally mathematical, terms. Friston's free energy principle supplies it. [A Markov blanket](record:f2a880e4-1995-4b18-912d-90daaf6dab4e) separates a system from what it is not, so [persisting means minimizing free energy](record:838e863a-a122-4003-b591-f3a47a37dcce), and [the Markov blanket yields selfhood and intentionality](record:131db7dd-2b87-46e1-a568-66edf24d4308). [Free energy, surprisal, uncertainty and arousal are one quantity](record:a8197b9e-ab89-42de-a45d-14ce00e685c6) seen in different vocabularies. [Affect is felt homeostatic deviation](record:8393b1d2-7d5a-462e-bdcf-33a93f8dfb6a), and the step from proto-mental to mental comes when many needs [must be compartmentalized and prioritized](record:9104bcdf-f459-48d2-ba82-0ed899de357c) in unpredicted contexts. [Precision weighting](record:d3ff30dd-b068-4745-a4c6-feb7e23f4782) does this, and [consciousness is felt uncertainty](record:31a7ba37-7364-4bdf-817f-66be0f393ece), the felt adjustment of precision, much like [the activity of Maxwell's daemon](record:932d3690-6e37-42d3-8b5d-c9285586d44e). It earns its keep in [choice under novelty](record:72c0c7ee-7a96-480e-8f4b-8d2d2642c1e7), and [cognition is the work that reduces affect](record:4103b767-7106-419e-943f-1e86f797f512), so [conscious cognition is predictive work in progress](record:83e230b4-1382-4222-9aec-031480d738a4) that fades once a task is automatized.

Critics answer that [the hydranencephaly evidence does not show consciousness](record:335cd451-0dd8-4e68-b361-03e417e15511), that [the brainstem lacks the coherence experience needs](record:e52b20ed-e841-45cd-9439-c74da3c8311e), and that [affect may need cortical re-representation](record:fcdc8eba-3313-4466-a710-a30a9dc22af7).
