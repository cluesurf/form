# Chronoflux, the plain account

Chronoflux begins from one law. Before there is any space, clock or force, a physical thing has to stay lawfully joined to itself as it changes, or nothing connects one moment of it to the next. Roy D. Herbert calls that law covariant continuity. In its barest form it is the statement that a current three-form on an oriented four-dimensional manifold is closed, $d\mathcal{J} = 0$, which needs an orientation and nothing else: no metric, no time and no observer. Once a metric is declared, the same law becomes the familiar conserved current $J^\mu = \rho_t u^\mu$ with $\nabla_\mu J^\mu = 0$, a temporal density carried along a timelike flow that can be moved around but never made or destroyed. An action for the current is one way to complete the law, not its source, since many different actions give the same continuity equation.

Everything else is recovered from that root by a ladder of admissibility conditions, each added because the step below it is no longer enough. A finite signal speed makes Galilean comparison between observers fail and leaves the Lorentz transformations. Uneven redistribution makes one global frame fail and leaves a metric, a connection and curvature. Conserved stress plus a divergence-compatible, second-order, local closure leaves the Einstein equations with a cosmological term. Writing the current as amplitude and phase gives a quantum potential and, for a coherent branch, the Schrödinger equation. The fraction of structure a finite observer can still recover, $R = I_{\mathrm{acc}} / I_{\mathrm{tot}}$, ties thermodynamics, decoherence and horizons together, a $3+1$ projection gives Navier-Stokes once residual terms are dropped, and the homogeneous regime gives FLRW cosmology, inflation and the Mukhanov-Sasaki equation. Time itself is what a positive, future-directed current carries, inertia is resistance to being moved through the temporal medium, and gravity is curvature answering the medium's stress. Herbert is explicit that the ladder reconstructs known physics as the minimal lawful completion at each rung rather than deducing it from continuity alone.

The method behind this is called Conservatio Continuum: a model is admissible only if it has a continuity relation, closes its conserved sectors, respects its symmetries and follows a variational or storage principle. In July 2026 Herbert rebuilt most of the program as Version 2.0 papers and graded every claim as open, conditional, closed, obstructed or superseded. Several earlier claims were withdrawn in that pass: the 2025 resolution of the Hubble tension, the parameter-free fine-structure constant, collapse as a relaxation of the flow, the regular limit of zero inertia, and the Millennium Problem resolutions, which became open projection routes. The framework says nothing about experience, mind or consciousness.

It is meant to be tested. Its pre-registered falsifiers are dispersive echoes in LIGO O4b and O5 ringdowns, one value of $\sqrt{z}$ read from both ringdown echoes and black hole evaporation, a residual Casimir torque across a superconducting transition, and a single parameter set that survives every observational arena at once. No source yet reports an outcome for any of them.
