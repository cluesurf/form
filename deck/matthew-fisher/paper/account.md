Matthew Fisher starts from the standard objection to any quantum brain: a warm, wet environment destroys quantum coherence far too fast to matter. He finds [one exception, nuclear spins](record:c882ebba-4730-4670-8480-77eeb406cd8a), and works backward by [reverse engineering](record:01d08e8d-1919-4751-af7f-7bdfb55b87a2) what quantum processing would need. A nucleus with spin one half has no electric quadrupole moment, so only weak magnetic fields disturb it, and among the common elements of biochemistry that singles out [phosphorus as the neural qubit](record:76d5b1ba-bd03-450d-a7d4-9aae368842a9). Phosphorus lives in the phosphate ion, which [carries the qubit](record:e7ea6ec2-1de3-40a9-b0df-d51f40e195d1) around a cell, and in the calcium phosphate cluster $\mathrm{Ca_9(PO_4)_6}$, the Posner molecule, which [stores it](record:d1792620-65c0-4e33-9f4f-14562d4f96d6). Because a Posner tumbles fast in water, the magnetic noise on its phosphorus spins averages out, and Fisher estimates [coherence for about a day](record:c74f2436-d854-4156-aa4a-9e2068676133).

The rest of the chain links spins to neurons. An enzyme that splits pyrophosphate into two phosphates [leaves the two phosphorus spins entangled](record:9e6e1d12-669a-487b-bdd5-3bdff35c3cd8), and Posners built from those phosphates inherit the entanglement. Fisher and Radzihovsky ground this step in a conjectured [Quantum Dynamical Selection rule](record:eb34663a-e5e2-4848-b078-83865a6dc0b3): reactions of small symmetric molecules cannot start from non-symmetric states, so [a bond-breaking enzyme performs a projective measurement](record:bbe0e043-48d3-4700-8471-34d669b13e17), and the rule [predicts effects in ordinary chemistry](record:3ef39c53-8030-4120-9941-0829c24fb29a). Posners [enter neurons during vesicle recycling](record:f98520d4-8bf9-4f6f-940f-50958e178bbe), and when two of them [bind and melt](record:b864e892-2dad-489b-9599-07904ffe91ec) they release calcium that makes a neuron more likely to fire. Entangled Posners in different neurons would then [correlate the firing of those neurons](record:423645c6-15dc-494c-999d-09eb2e5f69af), which is what Fisher means by quantum cognition. Lithium, if it replaces calcium inside a Posner, would disturb the spins, [a possible account of lithium therapy](record:d2a541f3-3c19-49ea-83ec-02765d49a948), and the two lithium isotopes [should then act differently](record:fd904be6-c18f-4d35-9f26-a15a305bc9ce).

Fisher [lists the experiments that could refute the chain](record:c2f9c939-a43e-4194-903d-787464abed5d), and the proposal [says nothing about experience](record:7ed04dc3-8d49-472e-8528-35e5b1ff1a95). Calculations by others have cut the expected lifetime: Player and Hore put [an upper bound of 37 minutes on Posner entanglement](record:0962adab-d8d9-45a3-b866-238668b19698), a later paper finds [entanglement between separate Posners decays in under a second](record:7158655b-acd9-4806-ab0f-9f8ddaa930a6), and Adams and colleagues find that [both lithium isotopes destroy the entanglement](record:ed195988-aaa4-41f3-a27c-d36b5810e798). On the other side, experiments show that [lithium isotopes change calcium phosphate mineralization differently](record:9416e409-5933-4e80-8e4d-140df5fbcb4a).
