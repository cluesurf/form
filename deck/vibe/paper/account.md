# Vibe Theory

Vibe takes experience as fundamental: discrete nodes of an experiential field. It explores whether physics, and all of reality, could emerge from a network of these experience nodes, updated by a relatively simple rule like a cellular automaton on a structured space. Its committed choices so far are few. Space is the four-dimensional hyperbolic honeycomb {3,4,3,4}, whose docks are 24-cells with one slot per direction ([space](record:3e791c75-5131-48c7-b552-2a4bb9feff6c)). Each slot holds a vibe, fear, calm or love (-1, 0, +1), with a role point, and each link holds a grid move and a flow ([the slot](record:701c617a-622b-4d2f-8068-f60c9937cc6a)). One update, the knit, runs every beat: a collision in every dock, then each slot streams to its neighbor ([the knit](record:12f3727e-0b31-4009-8b40-752ced848dfb)). The knit can be run backward exactly and keeps love minus fear constant ([reversibility](record:f0af02b4-357b-4420-a419-1fe5fb7b82d5)), and it draws no random number ([no chance](record:8970bc1c-5af7-42e4-a7e5-8d752dba9174)). Nothing else is base. Physics is meant to appear one to three emergent layers above the vibes, read on the three-dimensional husk of the four-dimensional mesh, never in the mesh's interior.

The program tests itself with about 1,600 deterministic experiments, and gathers a dozen results from them. Each result says how strong it is (derived, simulated, testable, tested, negative or unresolved), what kind of claim it is (a reproduction, an explanation, a retrodiction, a prediction or a measurement of the knit itself), and which committed choices it rests on. A result that rests on none of them, such as the SU(3) lattice gauge theory rebuilt from scratch ([the rebuild](record:795a7919-415d-450d-b7e8-a697c2ae6784)), is a reproduction: evidence that the tools work, not evidence that nature uses the knit.

Negative results are kept and shown. The bare knit, run without its fear beat, has no quantum sector of its own ([no amplitudes](record:e7cce525-ea93-418e-b81b-3557c5dbcc0d)), and the knit used before 2026-09-25 carried none of the strong force's color symmetry, while the knit adopted that day carries only a finite color group ([color](record:b28ef656-da29-48aa-a413-0dc2e3b53fbc)). The program also lists the objections that still stand against it, and every passage of its published papers that claimed more than the code it cites supports. No prediction has yet passed the program's own gate, so its prediction table is empty.
