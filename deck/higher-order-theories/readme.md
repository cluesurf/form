# Higher-Order Theories of Consciousness

Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted, unreviewed. Not written or endorsed by David Rosenthal, Hakwan Lau and Richard Brown. To propose a correction, open an issue or pull request that changes the record and cites the source.

This is a [form.surf](https://form.surf) model repository for the higher-order approach to consciousness: a mental state is conscious when one is aware of being in it, and that awareness is a distinct higher-order representation of the state. It holds David Rosenthal's higher-order thought (HOT) theory from philosophy, Hakwan Lau's empirical program that ties the higher-order representation to prefrontal monitoring of perceptual signals (the higher-order statistical inference view, later perceptual reality monitoring), the HOROR variant associated with Richard Brown, and Stephen Fleming's allied higher-order state space model. Each claim is one JSON record in `proposition/`, with its kind (definitional, empirical, philosophical), its epistemic status, and the source and page it was taken from. The claims about what makes a state conscious, about misrepresentation and about what it is like are philosophical. The claims about prefrontal cortex, blindsight, metacognition and inflated peripheral vision are empirical, and are marked by what the cited evidence supports. Findings reported by the authors from other laboratories are in `observation/`, each pointing at the paper that reports it, and `link/` joins claims to each other and to evidence. Published objections by Ned Block, Rafael Malach and Melanie Boly and colleagues, with the replies of Rosenthal, Josh Weisberg, Lau and Brian Odegaard, are kept as claims, the objections with `voice: "critique"`. A planned adversarial test of these theories against a first-order theory has no published outcome, and is recorded as pending.

Every record has `provenance.ai_written: true` and `reviewed: false` until a named person checks it against its sources.

Format: form.surf/1. Check with `form check .`.
