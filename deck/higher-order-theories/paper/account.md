# Higher-Order Theories of Consciousness

Higher-order theories start from one observation: [no mental state is conscious if one is wholly unaware of being in it](record:3421f4cb-f9c1-4cbc-9041-f7bd90c66d5b). The question they answer is about [state consciousness](record:3f5cd289-54c4-441d-a69e-79473631f89b), the difference between a thought, perception or feeling that is conscious and the same kind of state when it is not. Rosenthal turns the observation into [the transitivity principle](record:ee1a7b48-222e-4948-bfb7-6a444630034f): a state is conscious when one is conscious of it in a suitable way. This works only if [mental states can occur without being conscious](record:034b5424-ff11-472e-b2a6-7f19ad94c866), since otherwise [no informative explanation of consciousness is possible](record:fdc274c9-9fdc-4b7e-9181-1311b5597b0a).

The higher-order thought theory says how one is aware of one's conscious states. It is not by an inner sense, since [the awareness has no qualities of its own](record:1515c830-a8d9-4033-a1f1-7833457a7b2b), and not by a mere disposition, since [a disposition makes one aware of nothing](record:6dc2babe-a83f-47a1-bd22-34967d39c93c). It is by [a higher-order thought](record:1d5cd935-9669-43e5-8c47-bbea8ff46266), and [a state is conscious when such a thought accompanies it](record:70a0ccae-834c-4772-af1b-dae917635552). [Higher-order thoughts are themselves seldom conscious](record:2409bc18-7d74-42c0-a6cf-8ba4833c8ec8), and [introspection is the case where one is](record:64f4cc15-aec7-4838-9899-7f3d018f4794). The theory explains why [sincere reports track consciousness](record:4edc4664-147e-48b3-8dc8-504eb7f3d14b) and why [learning words changes how experiences are conscious](record:700c590b-e0dd-4887-b86c-23d6fd27cc1d). Mental qualities [can occur without being conscious](record:b176c087-5796-4b0d-8b7b-d9b074bfdf14) and are fixed by [a quality space](record:efb02e2e-c54f-49fd-ae18-5ccc8d2286c6), while [what it is like for one](record:6945356c-2120-40c7-8b0d-422a01262b93) is [how one's mental life appears](record:132c71de-c669-46e1-852a-8beeb49716ef). So [the content of the higher-order thought fixes what it is like](record:c64bc83f-4eee-4ac4-abf8-0289086634b0), [an empty higher-order thought yields a seeming](record:4c5f1c41-3dcf-4213-95b4-23df5bfaa522), and [consciousness misrepresents routinely](record:5a2feedd-077c-430d-a956-5ee61ed59ec4). Being conscious [adds little utility](record:33d48b8b-e793-4ee3-86e8-1e85c27423c7) to what the states do anyway.

Lau and Rosenthal map the [higher-order representation](record:8c137b74-de06-421c-8e1c-44dfe52c3710) onto [prefrontal and parietal cortex](record:c3b214c4-93cf-48f2-bb6a-a0c5a5d6ef23) and derive a test: [awareness can change while first-order states and task performance stay the same](record:d72d05a3-61ac-4188-bee9-1576b95b8673). [Relative blindsight in normal observers](record:43ae07ff-a976-4346-af6d-dbce47f09d7c) and [prefrontal TMS](record:2d988f20-8e65-45e8-b0b4-2ec7a3edebab) fit it. Lau's own variant treats awareness as [a higher-order judgment that a first-order signal is reliable](record:1f3050ed-426a-49e5-bb67-920e61558c18), which also explains [inflated perception at unattended locations](record:dc323e6c-4053-43c2-9a57-b10fd7f8646b) and reads the partial report results as [inflation rather than overflow](record:718f4b17-c655-45a3-9d43-55b132d6c400). Fleming's allied model makes [awareness a higher-order state in a generative model](record:5e05e78d-545b-48eb-ad82-92b7f8ff16b6) that [reproduces global ignition](record:d4f19f8b-a3f0-4a1b-85e4-f15126174fc4), and the [HOROR variant](record:549e5bc2-dc47-45b7-b2f5-4dd66af00aad) places phenomenal consciousness in the higher-order representation itself.

Block holds that [an empty higher-order thought makes the theory incoherent](record:02d06292-a2fd-4c0f-a01a-8f6d6c39afd3) and its [what it is like fake](record:1bcb0b9b-2ba5-499c-a703-246715e24230), and that [experience overflows access](record:331a588c-8225-43ab-8b40-d31c2d44fd9f). Malach asks about [absorbed states](record:d0f0f526-e42c-4ace-ac9c-77a4b1ef90a8). Boly and colleagues argue that [frontal lesions spare consciousness](record:c590fe10-0ecf-43ce-b3f1-074311a2f6d8) and [frontal stimulation changes no experience](record:10692f74-9d20-4f3e-8637-2bfbb46a9908), and Odegaard, Knight and Lau answer that [the prefrontal null findings do not falsify the view](record:23a219e4-f63f-4d06-b666-9a72eca5f5e8). An [adversarial collaboration](record:06ef4d46-a3d4-4b67-8b9a-a37f61a38e8a) set up to test higher-order against first-order theories has not reported an outcome.
