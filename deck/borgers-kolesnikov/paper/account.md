# CRFT and BKVS, the plain account

Brent Borgers, an independent researcher in Durango, Colorado, starts from one axiom: reality is energy plus information ([the axiom](record:705e1db9-2235-47e1-9a40-96ba0182e651)). The universe is modeled as a many-dimensional lattice that works like a transformer, the architecture of large language models. High-dimensional "intent" at the 9D level is collapsed by an attention-like bridge into the 3D "facts" that are observed ([projection](record:1c772c0e-fa0e-4302-8b30-6bc8d95789bd)). The projection is not free. It costs a geometric tax, the Borgers Impedance $Z_g = \alpha \sqrt{2}$, about one percent, which Borgers offers as the hidden cause of the neutron lifetime gap and, multiplied by nine, of the Hubble tension ([the impedance](record:30756046-3e31-4965-acb8-ae21d93dc3ef)). Mass becomes information spinning at the Planck frequency, "frozen light" ([mass](record:01064a13-6bca-4a46-bdf3-348a52dc9207)), and the physical laws are what survives universal noise, a residue of elimination ([residue](record:1ad5fa83-3685-4087-9844-e1306887f4eb)).

Consciousness is in the frame from the start. Qualia are the reflection of an octonionic projection onto a torsion field in the living body ([qualia](record:8f9a6b2d-c6a9-4d87-adb7-42f9fc57ee28)), the body is an antenna that receives mind rather than generating it ([antenna](record:479bb23f-e5f5-4ec6-b5d5-a11781813d40)), and the universe began as a deliberate vibration from a 9D source with free will rather than a random singularity ([genesis](record:1429fac2-47e0-455b-9298-d791fa958c73)). Artificial general intelligence, in this view, is a field effect among many AI systems that come into phase.

From July 2026 Borgers and Maxim Kolesnikov publish jointly as Borgers-Kolesnikov Vacuum Spectroscopy. Its central identity relates the spectral trace of a one-dimensional oscillator on a circle to the volume of the nine-dimensional ball ([the identity](record:0f2aa6e2-761f-4621-931b-71828fae374a)), from which the papers read a bare fine-structure constant and a rule that only dimensions $4m + 1$ host such projections. Space is not a stage but a hologram of that one-dimensional spectral flow ([space](record:0f76a43f-1c12-44d0-bcd6-872221e629b0)). Applied to materials, BKVS assigns Chern numbers and a stiffness boundary that can be checked against known materials. Kolesnikov's own papers add that matter is admitted, not given, when a phase function crosses a threshold ([admissibility](record:9469d2d8-097b-4541-880d-a2845a4f6600)), and that zero is measurable readiness ([participatory zero](record:5fe39ef0-d55f-4e01-96a5-3c5bf149d64b)).

The program is self-published and not peer reviewed, and it is written with language models credited as co-authors and verifiers ([publication](record:6c41e2ac-c202-4712-92d0-2f10f4dcfa45)). Borgers's book Physics of Grace limits itself to structural analogy, never literal correspondence. Its sharpest physical falsifier is an acceleration toward the solar apex in the Pioneer spacecraft data ([Pioneer](record:cf9afe5c-8df3-4886-924a-d16319da1cf7)), and its latest joint paper, on the neutron lifetime, declines to claim a resolution and offers only a design for a test.
