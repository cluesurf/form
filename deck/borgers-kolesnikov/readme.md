# Causal Recursion Field Theory and BKVS

**Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted (claude-opus-5-5), unreviewed. Not written or endorsed by Brent Borgers and Maxim Kolesnikov.**
To propose a correction, open an issue or a pull request that changes the record in question, citing the source and locator that shows the error. A correction from Brent Borgers and Maxim Kolesnikov is especially welcome.

Brent Borgers's Causal Recursion Field Theory (CRFT, also "Informational Physics"), from December 2025, and the joint program he and Maxim Kolesnikov have run since July 2026 as Borgers-Kolesnikov Vacuum Spectroscopy (BKVS), under the shared label "1188". CRFT holds that reality is energy plus information, projected from a 9D source down to the 3D world at a fixed geometric tax, the Borgers Impedance $Z_g = \alpha \sqrt{2}$, which it offers as the cause of the neutron lifetime gap and the Hubble tension. BKVS ties the spectra of high-dimensional representations to the elastic and topological properties of materials. Kolesnikov's own papers add admissibility: matter is admitted when a phase function crosses a threshold, and zero is readiness rather than absence. Myo Oo is named as a collaborator on BKVS in the joint announcement. No paper of his on BKVS was found.

The work is self-distributed on Zenodo, Substack and Academia.edu and is not peer reviewed. Several papers credit large language models as co-authors or verifiers (Claude, Gemini, Grok, Copilot and others). These facts are recorded in `form.json` under `framework.publication` and in the claims that cite them.

This is a form.surf model repository (format `form.surf/1`). It holds the framework's own claims, each as one JSON record in `proposition/`, and the relations between them in `link/`. Every record names its sources in `provenance.sources`, with a locator and, where it could be checked, a short verbatim quote. `paper/account.md` is the plain account.

## Labels

- `provenance.ai_written: true`: a model extracted the record from the cited source.
- `provenance.reviewed: false`: no person has yet read the record against its source.
- `epistemic_status: "hypothesized"`: every physical and numerical claim of the program. `inferred` marks only a stated mathematical identity or a fact about how the work is published.
- `prediction`: the apex-directed acceleration in Pioneer data, the program's one stated physical falsifier. Its `outcome` is null, since no source reports the reanalysis.
- There are no `observation/` records and no `voice: "critique"` claims: no source reports a test of a CRFT prediction, and no published criticism was found apart from one Substack comment, left out.
