# Recurrent Processing Theory

Recurrent processing theory starts from two modes of visual processing. In the first hundred milliseconds or so, [a feedforward sweep](record:c32f6d4e-12db-42f8-a95c-9189d25ee7c9) carries signals from the eyes through the visual areas and on to frontal and motor cortex, extracting features and categories. After that, [recurrent processing](record:a91095ff-0472-4e96-bea8-6e5b925d13fc) sets in, as horizontal and feedback connections carry signals from higher areas back to lower ones. Lamme's claim is that [the feedforward sweep is unconscious](record:886a22e5-5ade-488a-8113-a1975a704647) however deep it reaches, that [recurrent processing is needed for seeing](record:32325989-5e0b-431e-b8b7-a3ba0134ebb3), and that [recurrence confined to visual areas is already phenomenal experience](record:532ceb88-bd11-4104-9ad4-e3d054ccfee9), while [recurrence that reaches frontal and motor areas adds access](record:df913d93-6337-43f0-b209-4f96ca608b8a). The theory uses Block's terms, [phenomenal consciousness](record:45ea14c4-5e07-4b1a-9155-52df0ee0b617) and [access consciousness](record:2a4cd3d5-3d54-4ffb-88fc-5f5b5da723cb), and lays them out as [four stages of processing](record:8a4fc689-91c4-46e8-802f-be0f4cb0580d).

The evidence is mostly from vision. When a stimulus is masked, [early feedforward responses survive while later recurrent signals vanish](record:a74a44bf-ec2b-4c73-a388-0aad2760c0e4), and [figure-ground signals in V1 disappear when a figure is not seen](record:b7011f93-f3ba-4907-a5dd-862b0ae7d618) or [under anesthesia](record:ae93fe43-c1d5-42dc-aac1-ce08acc7d9f2). [Transcranial magnetic stimulation timed to the feedback from V5 to V1](record:68ac5f40-6ccc-4474-86f6-2374442b09a1) changes what is seen. Lamme's 2020 review sorts visual functions by stage and concludes that [perceptual organization, such as grouping and figure-ground segregation, marks the step to conscious seeing](record:8162d2a9-b3b1-4663-a32b-d2fba15a1a92), while [attention adds no visual operation](record:69682594-f49b-4d1e-a6a2-4142e5bc11c0). Because [attention and consciousness are separate processes](record:27788643-fd77-4093-9363-1efdbdc881b2), Lamme places [the line between unconscious and conscious seeing between stages 2 and 3](record:38e33a90-03c3-495d-b6f7-439bf2f61eee): an unattended stimulus is seen but not accessed. [Fragile visual short-term memory](record:afa8f79e-2101-4598-b5b2-987ba07b8c5d), a store that holds more than the four items of working memory for a few seconds, is read as [a window on seeing without access](record:97137e66-5a4c-4fe5-9416-9b278079f19b).

The theory rests on [a neural stance](record:25716c9d-d953-41e9-9e1f-c692adc69775): when report and other behavioral criteria conflict, arguments from neuroscience should settle what counts as conscious. Lamme argues that [what makes recurrence matter is recurrency itself, not the activation of frontal workspace neurons](record:599464a8-6b71-4a3b-997a-0f0bcfa4fb28), since [masked, unseen stimuli also activate prefrontal cortex](record:12580dd1-3b51-4d0f-9cf4-4fd5e4b6b579). Recurrence may matter because [it mediates plasticity and learning](record:848a16fd-a664-4318-9f85-ed618f979584), the candidate Lamme gives for [a missing ingredient](record:426d18f5-7fc5-4c54-804f-1d1a789ad611) once it is clear recurrence is common in the brain.

Critics answer that [a theory dividing experience from function cannot be tested](record:c2686f51-efb3-4a73-9d2f-4b645a05aed6), as Cohen and Dennett's [perfect experiment](record:9341b8ea-39e6-4101-b73e-22833f2dcf5a) is meant to show, that [partial report can be explained by unconscious storage](record:d4d3c098-1e3b-4968-8f23-0bf7b4d1508c), and that [strong sensory processing occurs without any experience](record:0eac1991-9e5a-4956-be1c-68b589f99513). Block, who agrees with Lamme's conclusions, holds that [similarity between processing stages is always similarity in a respect](record:bf2d8bf3-023c-4729-bd40-55e414f0ae0b). The 2024 multiscale review notes that [recurrence seems far more widespread than consciousness](record:163ee071-4b2b-41a1-a74e-47aa48414f51), and the unfolding argument holds that [structure theories cannot be told apart from feedforward equivalents](record:8db91e18-05d8-4b1e-80e7-5635c46ed037). An adversarial collaboration that [tests the theory against two higher-order theories](record:085ac288-cf60-4017-8bbe-e25993d3cafa) has not yet reported an outcome.
