# Eliminative Materialism

Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted, unreviewed. Not written or endorsed by Paul M. Churchland and Patricia S. Churchland. To propose a correction, open an issue or pull request that changes the record and cites the source.

This is a [form.surf](https://form.surf) model repository for eliminative materialism in the form Paul M. Churchland and Patricia S. Churchland gave it: commonsense psychology is a theory, the posits of that theory (beliefs, desires, and perhaps consciousness as the folk conceive it) may pick out nothing real, and the framework that replaces it will come from a matured neuroscience developed together with psychology. Paul Churchland's 1981 paper has no open copy, so its arguments are stated from the Stanford Encyclopedia entry. Patricia Churchland's papers and her 1986 book Neurophilosophy are quoted from the copies on her own site. Each claim is one JSON record in `proposition/`, with its kind, its epistemic status, and the source and page it was taken from. The self-refutation objection, the argument from the success of folk psychology and the view that folk psychology is not a theory are kept as claims with `voice: "critique"`, beside the replies the Churchlands give.

Related folders: [illusionism](../illusionism/) is the closest relative. Both deny that commonsense mental posits are real as conceived, both distrust introspection, and illusionists are, in the Stanford entry's words, eliminativists about phenomenal consciousness. This folder differs in its target, the propositional attitudes of folk psychology as a theory, and in its method, replacement by neuroscience rather than explanation of an illusion. [computational-functionalism](../computational-functionalism/) holds the Churchlands' 1981 defense of functionalism against the qualia objections. [epiphenomenalism](../epiphenomenalism/) holds the knowledge argument that Paul Churchland answered.

Every record has `provenance.ai_written: true` and `reviewed: false` until a named person checks it against its sources.

Format: form.surf/1. Check with `form check .`.
