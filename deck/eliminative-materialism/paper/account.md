# Eliminative Materialism

[Eliminative materialism](record:e480c33b-cd3d-4662-ac02-335c4fbba66a) holds that some commonsense mental states, beliefs and desires above all, do not exist. It rests on two claims. First, [folk psychology is a theory](record:15c9abd8-8cf0-4081-9544-d54b1b491317), with [laws and posits](record:aa8ae7d2-0814-4e12-9d10-07a28e6fe966), so how good it is is an empirical question. Second, it is [radically false](record:dd61b004-94f3-4114-916e-3b00e19706c0), so its posits face [elimination, not reduction](record:d3983bab-ee87-4ee6-840c-e811689413d3), as demons were eliminated while light was reduced. What remains is that [the mind is nothing more than what the brain does](record:d70e1555-c75b-442f-8169-af67754d27ee), described by [a matured neuroscience](record:7a5d895e-2ae1-453c-a665-e446c6ae1eda).

The arguments are that folk psychology is [stagnant and explains little](record:22845294-f75c-4442-82f3-d0558b1bd2b8), that [folk theories of everything else were wrong](record:f76efb42-ec57-406b-aceb-e9882251eff3), that [nothing in the brain looks like a sentence](record:161cfe5f-73ba-460c-bf79-90894da384a4), as [whales with melodic attitudes](record:6e21e131-530d-42d2-a312-0e79354d190e) would mistake their own communication for their cognition, and that [introspection is theory-laden](record:a081004f-ebd8-4618-af6c-20ddd7357e56). Patricia Churchland extends this to consciousness: [the concept may come unstuck](record:374beb96-56e8-41d6-a04d-a138b58409e6), since [introspection is not transparent](record:4dadbc85-bea2-4ac8-a304-591700327a1f), [blindsight](record:e8dbdebd-02f1-4d16-b66e-12443e8bf2c8), [blindness denial](record:243e478d-d4c6-40e2-bce6-6b6d1a9866d5) and [confabulation](record:c3564c5d-fe9d-4fe8-946c-7abef7ef090e) show awareness and report coming apart, and [there is no pontifical neuron](record:bde88ff2-efa2-4428-8f55-e81ec194a204). Her method is [co-evolution of neuroscience and psychology](record:3023aa10-3982-4fdb-b40d-5354ccf0005f), with [no discarding of folk psychology before a successor exists](record:4af2205c-0583-4965-a1d5-8cc552b5b5f4), and against the hard problem she holds that [ignorance is no premise](record:a6bb55b3-9a94-470e-a73c-2971e0e34ad9).

Critics answer that the view [refutes itself](record:4c2e7c9b-2abb-4397-8ef5-48623289294c), that [folk psychology succeeds too well to be false](record:d4692d49-fc94-4644-9d35-d6424eb2abc6), that [it is not a theory at all](record:019fb4c8-fb89-4571-93d2-806671745155), and that it [needs no sentences in the head](record:38822ec5-a00c-4d24-8cd9-5ebdb06dbb57). The Churchlands reply that [self-refutation begs the question](record:8810a460-627a-466f-a08a-e3dd12a609a0) and that [success can hide a false theory](record:f51f9b5f-3782-4a0f-970d-5b7f1b859c11). Illusionism is [eliminativism about phenomenal consciousness](record:c3db2ff4-36e2-423e-860a-24d9df733210).
