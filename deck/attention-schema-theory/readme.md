# Attention Schema Theory

Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted, unreviewed. Not written or endorsed by Michael S. A. Graziano. To propose a correction, open an issue or pull request that changes the record and cites the source.

This is a [form.surf](https://form.surf) model repository for the Attention Schema Theory (AST) of Michael S. A. Graziano: the brain builds a simplified, automatic model of its own selective attention, the attention schema, in the way it builds a body schema to control the body. The model helps control attention, is also used to model the attention of other people, and because it lacks any detail about neurons or signal competition, it depicts attention as a nonphysical essence that takes vivid possession of things. AST holds that this depiction, accessed by higher cognition, is why people believe and claim that they have subjective experience. Each claim is one JSON record in `proposition/`, with its kind (definitional, empirical, philosophical), its epistemic status, and the source and page or section it was taken from. Sources read from PubMed Central XML have no page numbers and are cited by section. Experiments on the beam model of gaze, the temporoparietal junction and neural network agents are in `observation/`, including independent work by other groups, and `link/` joins claims to each other and to evidence. Published objections, including those AST answers, are kept as claims with `voice: "critique"`.

Every record has `provenance.ai_written: true` and `reviewed: false` until a named person checks it against its sources.

Format: form.surf/1. Check with `form check .`.
