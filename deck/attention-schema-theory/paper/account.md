# Attention Schema Theory

AST begins with [selective attention](record:d98add4e-00a3-41c9-98ac-138bc4ee1afb), a competition among signals in which a few win and dominate the brain's downstream processing. A principle of control engineering is that [a good controller contains a model of what it controls](record:ef87f373-0090-4bf6-856b-2cfd1b41e38b), so the brain is proposed to build [an attention schema](record:4e35e155-d252-4816-a8ba-15bf5b865a6c), a descriptive and predictive model of attention, [in parallel to the body schema](record:90c67235-cde5-4a56-afad-0a268e987db2). [Its first job is control](record:5bea01f5-7d73-4407-b571-61abc69a4f9a), and the theory's central claim is that [subjective awareness is that internal model of attention](record:e3e44d34-6715-4762-8ce1-3efdcd8b3a60).

Two general principles carry the argument from control to consciousness. [Every belief and claim depends on information in the brain](record:e78e5752-820f-44d0-842e-a62c8a216307), and [the brain's models are never accurate](record:aaf7f1c4-68f4-47cb-b34c-3e6e6d38745b). Most accounts [conflate having consciousness with believing one has it](record:5595ca42-760c-4dcd-b7eb-06e2e4414955). The attention schema [lacks any detail about neurons or synapses](record:59510573-1c3e-4a8a-bb15-cf3023beedba), so it [depicts a physically incoherent possession of things](record:0b49e350-0d1e-4e8e-b324-b739138b652f), and a brain reporting from it says it has an ethereal awareness. By Graziano's account [the theory explains the claim, not a feeling](record:f0b533c0-5124-4306-a1ee-116e3d95ee96), and he prefers to call consciousness [a caricature of attention rather than an illusion](record:ddab98b3-05e6-448f-9599-b29f769c7ffb).

The theory predicts that awareness and attention [usually covary but can come apart](record:7953903d-36bd-4b09-a3f9-9a52143bc62a), mostly [at the threshold of detection](record:7858af9e-3f6a-4d1b-9afc-5f64f8e90386), and that [without awareness, attention remains but its control fails](record:daed49d9-48a3-4978-8456-07a83e3d4ff2). Experiments with cues outside awareness [fit that prediction](record:cb677fab-3e35-44bd-a37d-13f9cf7e59bc), and in a neural network agent [an attention schema made the control of attention possible](record:6b6bd39f-1eed-4297-8149-10a2767009ef). In fMRI, [the right temporoparietal junction was active where awareness allowed control](record:39523bba-e267-4d66-89e8-8d5c318056fa), consistent with [the TPJ as a node in an attention schema network](record:746231e2-d439-4134-aed5-11c41d9ceaeb).

The same schema is [used to model the attention of others](record:a015f1a4-c9de-42a7-bd73-96eb95f5bf0b). People [treat gaze as a gentle force-carrying beam](record:532f403c-8dcb-4d8b-a547-4f5c52ee45d8), which AST reads as [an implicit, simplified model of other people's attention](record:3eb0b972-b63c-457b-8da8-cf05a6fb834c). In 2024 Graziano frames the theory inside illusionism, [rejects hard illusionism](record:5f91f2e3-4fd9-43ca-9fd1-f8d930e98eab), argues that [theories lacking an "Arrow B" do not explain reports](record:f366e70f-732f-468e-9683-fba4f86e2943), and proposes that [AST is an illusionized higher-order theory](record:6cb6a9db-db88-45c6-a5d1-5536e48c64e0) and that [AST and an illusionist global workspace are one theory](record:2ed7e3d4-d96e-48f6-9a41-114249b5740a), both views of [deep selective processing](record:96aa8c3d-66da-4693-a012-583df03899c2). If the theory is right, [artificial consciousness is buildable](record:8dbc7630-278a-4196-9b64-48bffb21e5bc).

David Chalmers holds that [AST says little about why the model depicts awareness as ethereal](record:4f8a9fb4-3e11-4683-83c6-1b51460c85b4), doubts that [attention is the right relation to model](record:2d624267-1211-4b64-af41-d82a55237ddd), and argues that [weak illusionism leaves the hard problem in place](record:f736227f-e32c-4f57-9c53-c6601d764ec0). Others say [AST explains only the belief in consciousness](record:91c00719-3b8d-43c1-acad-19dd8bb4c7fb). Graziano and Schurger answer that [AST explains attention control and social modeling as well](record:22ee8cf3-4768-4cf4-ad39-31b954a09ef6) and that [it reframes consciousness rather than explaining it away](record:677b9e4b-bf9e-4e2d-a8d2-7d26f9f64ab1). An independent [topological argument](record:5a4a576d-52a2-429f-81b4-a55641c44b17) supports the claim that the schema must be incomplete.
