# The Free Energy Principle, in plain words

Karl Friston starts from an unusual question. Most of biology asks what a
living thing must do in order to stay alive. The [free energy principle](record:4fa3bb7b-afdc-4a53-bd29-14eff8f2a27b)
asks the reverse: if something exists as a thing, distinct from its
surroundings for long enough to be recognized, what must it be doing? The
answer begins with a boundary. A [Markov blanket](record:c258c2e8-34b1-4ab0-ba23-eddafddeecad) is
the set of states (senses and actions, for an animal) through which the
inside of a thing and the outside affect each other only indirectly. Any
system with such a blanket that keeps returning to its characteristic
states can be described as if its [internal states encode beliefs](record:f9bffa23-3e9e-4811-ad6a-06bdd3048a14)
about the outside world, and as if it acts to keep its [surprisal](record:5bc31f2e-ed95-4825-a1a6-85824a0a3af8),
the improbability of what it senses, low. The quantity it is described as
minimizing is [variational free energy](record:8f838e96-4057-4e1c-ab99-96320386ab1d), an upper
bound on surprisal that the system can compute from its own states.

The principle is mathematics, and Friston says so: it is [not falsifiable as a mathematical statement](record:a37e77dc-0c53-46d0-b71d-10abf84119d2),
in the way that the principle of least action is not falsifiable. It
becomes testable when one commits to a [specific generative model](record:e7827c9f-e6f9-4c18-a8b4-16e1ad096361)
of a particular system. Those commitments are the process theories.
[Active inference](record:6f769e90-0601-4e50-b3b7-8d1d82e4d1c2) says that perception (changing beliefs to fit
the senses) and action (changing the senses to fit beliefs) minimize the
same quantity, and that choices follow [expected free energy](record:51075255-9e90-4ad6-9056-9e66b95c297a),
which rewards both reaching preferred outcomes and resolving uncertainty.
Predictive coding in the cortex is one such process theory: [cortical responses](record:de4ece67-abb5-4cf5-9f67-d376a867e846)
carry prediction errors, which predicts hierarchical, reciprocal wiring and
the mismatch negativity. A 2023 experiment found that [cultured cortical networks](record:0a31dfdd-b3d4-4909-a1cc-537f06584398)
reorganize as free energy minimization predicts.

The same account reaches life and mind. Friston argues that [life emerges](record:c9586f0d-05ee-4412-a351-3283a6d4e9c0)
in any ergodic system with a Markov blanket, that living systems are
[Markov blankets of Markov blankets](record:3fedb309-47a7-439a-a09f-dfcd2e0a2df9), and that [all biological processes](record:c0876ba3-9b6a-43c1-9193-32ffb865784c)
can be read as inference. Consciousness, on his view, is not that
inference as such: it is inference with [temporal depth](record:0a4a5239-082a-440f-8035-78e5625314d5)
about the consequences of one's own actions. A virus self-evidences but is
[not conscious](record:4610b45e-613d-4bbd-a417-863a0b66aa70). Mind and matter are two readings of one
blanketed system, the position he calls [Markovian monism](record:777abcd8-0e34-432c-a292-4017e62e2182).

Critics question the step from mathematics to metaphysics. Some argue
that Markov blankets as [physical boundaries](record:0af8f6e7-b038-4ca5-a135-74215d4489cf) need
premises the mathematics cannot supply, that the [original lemma](record:8b5b4738-1b5d-49bf-a476-51c5ed1a140f)
fails as first stated, and that the required [conditions are narrow](record:4178c689-bab8-4711-a7fe-3286c7479ead).
Those objections are recorded here as open claims beside the framework's own.
