# Free Energy Principle

Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted, unreviewed. This
repository is not written or endorsed by Karl Friston or his
collaborators. Every record was extracted by a model (`claude-opus-5-5`)
from the papers listed in `source/`, and no person has yet checked a
record against its source. To propose a correction, open an issue or a
pull request that changes the record and cites the page or section of
the source.

This is a form.surf model repository (format `form.surf/1`) stating Karl
Friston's free energy principle and active inference in their own terms:
the principle as a mathematical claim, the process theories as empirical
claims, the account of life, self and consciousness, and the published
criticisms as open claims.

- `paper/account.md`: the framework in plain words
- `proposition/`: one claim per file. `voice: "critique"` marks a
  published criticism
- `observation/`: the evidence the framework cites and the evidence against it
- `link/`: how claims depend on and bear on each other
- `source/`: the papers, each with a DOI, PubMed id or arXiv id

Check it with `form check deck/free-energy-principle` from the root of the form repository.
