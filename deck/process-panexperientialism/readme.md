# Process Panexperientialism

Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted, unreviewed. Not written or endorsed by Alfred North Whitehead and David Ray Griffin. To propose a correction, open an issue or pull request that changes the record and cites the source.

This is a [form.surf](https://form.surf) model repository for process panexperientialism: Alfred North Whitehead's philosophy of organism, set out in *Process and Reality* (1929), read as a theory of consciousness, with David Ray Griffin's case that it is a nonreductive "panexperientialist physicalism". Its sources are the 1929 printing of *Process and Reality* (page locators refer to that printing, not to the 1978 corrected edition most secondary work cites), the Stanford Encyclopedia entry on Whitehead by Ronny Desmet and Andrew David Irvine, Griffin's 1999 critique of Jaegwon Kim with Kim's reply, Matthew David Segall's comparison of Whitehead with the analytic panpsychist revival, and Tam Hunt and Jonathan W. Schooler's resonance theory of consciousness, which starts from a Whiteheadian panpsychism. Griffin's book *Unsnarling the World-Knot* (1998) has no open copy and was not read. Each claim is one JSON record in `proposition/`, with its kind, its epistemic status, and the source and locator it was taken from. The terms of the scheme are marked designed, its theses hypothesized, what follows from them inferred, and Hunt and Schooler's conjectures empirical and hypothesized. Objections, chiefly Kim's, are kept as claims with `voice: "critique"`. Whitehead's theology is part of his system but is left out here. Related model folders: `panpsychism` (the analytic substance-property view, which Segall contrasts with this one), `cosmopsychism` (Segall reads Whitehead as neither a micropsychist nor a cosmopsychist exclusively), `russellian-monism` (Segall denies that experience is an intrinsic property of matter in the Russellian sense), `quantum-event-ontology` (Gambini and Pullin, who use Whitehead's prehension for quantum events), [`analytic-idealism`](../analytic-idealism/) (Bernardo Kastrup's view that experience is fundamental as one universal consciousness, where this view has many momentary occasions), and [`vibe`](../vibe/). Vibe also takes experience as fundamental, as discrete nodes of an experiential field.

Every record has `provenance.ai_written: true` and `reviewed: false` until a named person checks it against its sources.

Format: form.surf/1. Check with `form check .`.
