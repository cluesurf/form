# Process Panexperientialism

The final realities, for Whitehead, are [actual occasions](record:db9687dd-2fe3-4cc9-a8fd-9dfbd8367d90), and [actual entities are drops of experience](record:f8ccd994-92d3-4cc1-a373-b087998330c5), from God to the most trivial puff of existence in empty space. Nothing acts but them: [the ontological principle](record:4e928947-847d-48c7-ba6f-af007024861b) says that without an actual entity there is no reason. An occasion is made of [prehensions](record:b9434e67-2d17-45dc-a709-26c8e08b53c6), feelings of earlier occasions with a subject, a datum and a subjective form, and it comes to be by [concrescence](record:b0431b35-a1ab-4756-9edb-1b7773a9c18c): the many become one, and are increased by one. [How an occasion becomes is what it is](record:04776d3d-af9f-44fa-b5ef-bfbda01c4670). Each is a [subject-superject](record:f0b700a7-c8f9-41c4-9cfb-9f189b5ce172) that [perishes subjectively and lives on objectively](record:15904045-96e1-4920-9324-cded77ed831e), and [becoming is atomic](record:f4698524-8e36-4d14-9c7b-be14363cddd5). The scheme [repudiates vacuous actuality](record:6a9d7197-535e-4b03-b2fa-85a2a1eda7e4): apart from the experiences of subjects there is nothing.

This makes the view [panexperientialism](record:7321f321-9f5e-46b8-a872-9e558c60065e), not panpsychism in the usual sense, because [consciousness presupposes experience](record:0f768160-723a-4940-9b2c-7c309442f915). [Most experience is not conscious](record:88461866-28e6-431b-8e72-fd5e67b06436), and consciousness is [the subjective form of feeling a contrast between fact and might-be](record:71a01f0e-8118-4e84-bbe0-08000a840e24), the crown of experience, only occasionally attained. [Every actuality is bipolar](record:8f4e1f9d-befd-451a-bc01-e4bd6620838d), physical and mental, and [the mental pole derives from the physical](record:0da52a78-87b7-4211-9b4b-de179e5a7e1c). A rock is an [aggregate with no overall experience](record:b1f5c140-b5a5-4fce-b1e9-b5b28144a207), an animal a [compound individual](record:0c24d93a-fe0f-40ed-adbd-9c5c1e982f59). [All the life in the body is the life of its cells](record:757ef93b-4e72-4aba-a41c-f3274cdebf3a), and the human mind is [a route of presiding occasions](record:327f86b7-6bce-4f55-a591-af617926345f) that wanders through the brain.

Griffin argues that this is [a form of physicalism](record:eeb69619-968f-45f6-8bce-41fe0dea5a08) whose stuff is [experiential creativity, an enlargement of energy](record:e5785968-f5b8-4254-9366-25d7a2bf6dea), so that [mental causation is real](record:872ef2b7-0653-47bf-a6f8-f0d4437bfe6e), occasions have [some self-determination](record:93fa4370-f652-4004-8c6f-89b403adf917) though [no absolute freedom](record:c3096fd0-db6e-4062-b755-2d58e9cf1e33), and [laws of nature are habits](record:c3c73c12-98c5-4e6f-9bda-542430df3b66), [statistical where individuals act](record:80902512-8713-488d-b58c-f013db827c7c). He holds, after Whitehead, that [what practice presupposes is not negotiable](record:add74a67-0143-4341-8623-b9b4b7b9fcd2). Segall argues that the view [does not make experience an intrinsic property of matter](record:a829f170-1a68-4b5b-a5c1-9989aaafb2a7), [reframes the combination problem](record:d5ee47bd-613b-497d-bd12-4d46498ede46) as [cumulative concrescence](record:ce4284f5-b684-4f73-884e-e751cd65866c), and is [neither micropsychist nor cosmopsychist](record:9e70afe5-e26e-430c-a43f-68caf5cf2c15). Hunt and Schooler start from [a Whiteheadian panpsychism](record:8b061bc3-4aa3-47fb-bc9b-34066743a3ea) and conjecture that [shared resonance combines conscious entities](record:01d350cc-b06b-41d7-84e0-475058052302) into [nested wholes that keep their parts](record:b56ccf5d-63b3-4197-a699-cfcf8f31cdf8), with [a test in conscious and unconscious states](record:a673c514-556c-49bd-8adf-5aed22f93358).

Kim answers that the foundation [reads like a metaphysical wish list](record:62e08479-d3e7-495a-9a6f-bfa7d1132d1f), that [proto-mentality does not explain human consciousness](record:7fcf029b-5533-43fd-90f4-a19f805bd05c) without a principle of combination, that [practical needs have no metaphysical priority](record:2e2ca46f-026f-44af-997b-ca6c26cdadb0), and that the view [is no form of physicalism](record:64304afe-5475-428f-9b63-e0af412be305). Deacon, as Segall reports, argues that [micro-intentionality adds nothing](record:6cfa6c40-05c9-4e3f-8642-e39e80dbdab2) once organization does the explaining. Whitehead treats [his categories as tentative](record:19e24d53-fd51-4708-b075-6922aa94ddde).
