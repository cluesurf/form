# Enactivism

Enactivism starts from life rather than from computation. Maturana and Varela defined a living cell by its organization: [a boundary and a reaction network that produce each other](record:2d7b8026-c1d2-4fbc-b80b-82dda5ab0734). Thompson and his coauthors generalize this to [autonomy](record:39cb8eeb-5f22-497f-a607-d75a0b464851), a self-producing network that is open to matter and energy and keeps its identity under precarious conditions. An autonomous system has a perspective, and from it the environment takes on value. [Sense-making](record:ae5ba3d8-c258-499f-a4ad-1c86609ef017) is this relational side of autonomy, and its stock example is a bacterium swimming up a sugar gradient, for which [sugar is food only relative to its metabolism](record:305266e1-bfd0-4e10-8445-17fee73926fc). Hence the slogan that [living is sense-making](record:7f42a296-690a-4bfd-8443-0ff69c2311da), the [co-emergence of a self and its world](record:1775b749-168a-45ca-891c-c0aa9a69b825), and the [deep continuity of life and mind](record:9b49970b-caf6-457b-8154-0ac363284981). Autopoiesis alone gives only an all-or-nothing norm of staying alive, so [adaptivity must be added](record:9457b8cf-6206-47a0-9c97-ecc930fc507e) for graded sense-making.

[Cognition is then the relation between organism and world](record:43cf4971-d52f-4fad-aabd-1835eeb278f7), not an event inside the head, and [emotion is part of it from the start](record:41c51992-ff98-46d6-a234-f042dd9c1c0a). The 1991 book [The Embodied Mind](record:a94febfb-3554-4552-b791-953042a9aceb) rejected [traditional notions of representation and computation](record:32515e3e-b1d1-486a-8bb5-e02456de82c2) and proposed a back-and-forth between science and disciplined experience.

For consciousness, [Thompson and Varela propose](record:40f820a1-65a9-4783-9012-075a3d826305) that there may be no minimal neural correlate whose intrinsic properties suffice for experience. Its best neural description is [large-scale dynamical signatures of synchrony](record:e6392a12-4855-42c2-ad45-37c61ba4a807), and conscious acts are [emergent processes](record:a93ae577-3428-412c-93c8-fc4f52c741df) that act back on local neural activity by [reciprocal causation](record:4d5551e9-18cb-4e2e-b663-f9db1eb9c228). The brain takes part in [three cycles of operation](record:123e6c54-cc09-4136-b593-8887ad2c6c4f), and [sentience is the feeling of being alive](record:b8f1fe99-cb32-4d5e-9a30-489186ce51d1). Thompson and Cosmelli turn the brain in a vat into a test: [any vat that worked would be a surrogate body](record:bc5eec7b-c680-4380-ae75-e78446441708), so [the minimal system for consciousness is an organism](record:76fbe087-7f98-40a6-9ff8-11e0c267cc2a).

The method is [neurophenomenology](record:1a863ad4-e2ad-4f66-84ce-0faa52990251): [first-person methods](record:ce962556-be5b-4154-b7b6-54bb72b0e35b) train subjects to describe their experience, and those descriptions and brain measures are meant to [constrain each other](record:233bb537-bb60-41d9-a488-74fde2c02bb2). In the pilot study, [reported readiness sorted trials into clusters with distinct synchrony patterns](record:330e709c-8d26-4f8e-879e-59e5898a7070). The aim is to [bridge the explanatory gap, not close it by reduction](record:a61c325b-6b2c-421f-a99b-9e2192a0ea6e), and Thompson recasts the hard problem as [the body-body problem](record:c58122e4-04fc-49dc-bfe3-198c6df62ab7), in which [experience is irreducible because every theory presupposes it](record:177fe149-4e21-443e-af53-dbd7b9067ea9).

The main critiques are that [organizational closure gives a system no direction](record:b86c40e5-77f6-47a0-b27c-2c4874ec9628), that [selective coupling does not make a candle seek oxygen](record:bf6f7650-4541-4942-a74f-6a5d7915d351), that [self-determination holds for every physical system](record:103f11ac-1a49-4074-97e6-c85fa2cb7932), that ["life is cognition" conflates cognition with adaptation](record:2f558c8a-9906-4d11-876e-412a5b7f9011), and that [first-person reports are unreliable](record:cd238be0-7169-4ca1-8f8a-6f31a091f5ed). Enactivists in turn argue that [the free energy principle is in tension with enaction](record:b9fe83cf-85e3-48ed-becd-2e3237595167). Whether [all life is sentient](record:2998f2ff-eb1f-4a87-acab-27352522f5bb) is a question Thompson left open in 2022, after [restricting consciousness to nervous systems in 2004](record:0afc25dd-1de1-44bb-a141-22d65be64a8c).
