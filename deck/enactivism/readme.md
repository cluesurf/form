# Enactivism

Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted, unreviewed. Not written or endorsed by Francisco Varela, Evan Thompson, Eleanor Rosch and Humberto Maturana. To propose a correction, open an issue or pull request that changes the record and cites the source.

This is a [form.surf](https://form.surf) model repository for enactivism, the enactive approach to mind of Francisco Varela, Evan Thompson and Eleanor Rosch, built on the theory of autopoiesis of Humberto Maturana and Varela. Its claims: a living system is autonomous, producing and sustaining itself, and its activity brings forth a world of significance, so living is sense-making and mind is continuous with life. Consciousness is a process of the whole organism coupled to its environment, studied by neurophenomenology, which pairs disciplined first-person reports with measures of large-scale brain synchrony. Each claim is one JSON record in `proposition/`, with its kind, its epistemic status, and the source and page it was taken from. The founding books (The Embodied Mind, Mind in Life, Autopoiesis and Cognition) are cited by ISBN for their main theses, and most quotes come from open papers by Thompson and his coauthors. The pilot experiment of Lutz and colleagues and the epilepsy studies of Varela's group are kept as observations. Objections, including the argument of Villalobos and Ward that organizational closure gives no direction or purpose, are kept as claims with `voice: "critique"`.

Every record has `provenance.ai_written: true` and `reviewed: false` until a named person checks it against its sources.

Format: form.surf/1. Check with `form check .`.
