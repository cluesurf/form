# Quantum Information Panpsychism

Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted, unreviewed. Not written or endorsed by Federico Faggin and Giacomo Mauro D'Ariano. To propose a correction, open an issue or pull request that changes the record and cites the source.

This is a [form.surf](https://form.surf) model repository for quantum information panpsychism, the view of Federico Faggin, worked out formally with the physicist Giacomo Mauro D'Ariano: consciousness is a system's experience of its own information, the information of experience must be quantum because experience cannot be shared, and quantum randomness that no knowledge can remove is free will. Each claim is one JSON record in `proposition/`, with its kind (definitional, empirical, mathematical, philosophical), its epistemic status, and the source and page it was taken from. The four principles and what follows from them are kept apart from Faggin's own metaphysics of One and consciousness units, which he calls highly speculative, and every claim of the latter is marked hypothesized. The theory's proposed experiments are empirical and untested. Opposing views the sources answer are kept as claims with `voice: "critique"`.

Every record has `provenance.ai_written: true` and `reviewed: false` until a named person checks it against its sources.

Format: form.surf/1. Check with `form check .`.
