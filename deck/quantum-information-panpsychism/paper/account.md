# Quantum information panpsychism (Faggin and D’Ariano)

Faggin and D'Ariano begin from Chalmers's hard problem and take the panpsychist answer in an informational form: [consciousness is a system's experience of its own information state and processing](record:c3e1d8bb-8214-45c9-9318-6b507951f1d0), a fundamental feature of information as mass and charge are of physics. Physics [supervenes on quantum information](record:63648571-14be-474f-9a1e-a6994fcc3181), and quantum information on consciousness.

The kind of information matters. [Experience cannot be shared, even in principle](record:f6074995-6362-4600-b0ac-402b4369db77). Sharing information is the same as copying it, copying is the same as reading without disturbance, and only classical information can be read that way, so [the information of consciousness is quantum](record:1c258481-67f5-45d5-bca9-9e8a1cd8865f). Experience is definite, one experience at a time, so [the conscious state is pure](record:9b435a53-9f64-480e-8b8c-3b54912e630a), an [ontic state distinct from the mixed epistemic state](record:b204b987-9817-4fde-b141-942cf7c45635) an outside observer would assign. [Qualia are pure quantum states](record:bcc33562-0b6b-4d7d-8a7c-eae04425b813), and entanglement builds thoughts out of them.

A pure state needs an evolution that keeps it pure, an atomic quantum operation, and such an operation is generally probabilistic. Its outcome, which [no knowledge could predict](record:b6a1aedd-db61-4d72-927f-b4be354b29f3), is free will. Purity also gives [a criterion for which systems are one mind](record:19040b07-81e2-4ee2-b0cb-d771bd45fbc5): a composite in a pure state under a pure transformation, with no subsystem as such, so that minds join only by quantum interaction. [Memory is classical](record:0c105d51-91cb-41c0-8109-dfba42aac940), while a short quantum buffer builds each moment of experience. The authors propose [tests](record:fe035a3e-1f1f-4355-9673-f98c16626440): complementarity and nonlocality in experience, and a count of qubits from memory recall.

Faggin sets this inside a wider picture he calls [highly speculative](record:3e7f0e85-bbc5-40ad-a978-0d985f15263b): [One](record:dc432f57-92c9-4254-ad9f-e821655b6779), the totality of what exists, comes to know itself through [consciousness units that exist before space, time, matter and energy](record:b3ef8207-3e73-4e17-a23b-726f146ad0d8), each with [an inner private semantic reality and an outer public symbolic one](record:f3f9bb18-0b31-4936-842d-12be090c03b1). Quantum fields are [the outer face of organizations of these units](record:f2e0cf1c-6967-451d-a508-d142a3f855f9). A self is a coherent combination of units, a living organism can host one, and [a computer cannot](record:f51382dc-35be-43d9-ae86-3a461a4db911), however complex, which runs against [predictions of conscious machines](record:5aea5d22-57af-4cad-a06f-397af1bffc60).
