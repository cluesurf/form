# Quantum interactive dualism (Stapp)

Stapp starts from a conflict. Classical physics is local and deterministic, and if it were the whole story [a person would be an automaton](record:767016c8-7310-4813-8a41-0be1e1acadb2) whose thoughts do nothing. He holds that the conflict comes from a theory known to be wrong at the relevant point: [setting Planck's constant to zero removes the fine structure where mind acts on matter](record:24532776-1503-4830-a564-120c569ba3e3).

Quantum theory as von Neumann wrote it evolves a state by [two different processes](record:acdc26a8-15be-42a2-8166-ecc63df15698). Between events the Schrodinger equation runs deterministically. At an event a question is posed, a yes-or-no projection $P$, and nature answers with Born-rule probabilities. [No known law fixes which question is posed, or when](record:4a52afff-1805-4ba5-84e4-7993e7b9b60b), so the causal closure of the physical [fails in quantum theory](record:eb5e8b48-b41b-43f5-ad68-ee6cb32e1b86). Stapp places mind there: [the choice of question is made by the experiential side of the person](record:95564fbf-0916-4c9f-9036-b67a3d23cbd3), [the observer is a body, brain and mind inside the quantum system](record:588c42c9-2363-4d6e-b445-a9b99766b829), and [each event joins a physical and a mental line of causation](record:d535502d-f272-4568-8246-1d64e1991079).

A single question does little. Repetition does more: by [the quantum Zeno effect](record:ef10c06c-70e9-4e0f-8983-f246691b4b6d), a question posed again and again in quick succession holds the state in the subspace where the answer is yes. Stapp proposes that [mental effort raises the rate of questioning](record:0a8d6d8b-e3d4-4a61-a06a-914a922d4670) and so [holds a template for action in place](record:1a7a7343-2305-40bc-adb5-5f02ced03398), which matches William James's account of will as attention held fast. Because the Zeno effect [works as well on a decohered mixture as on a pure state](record:6bb8b5c5-5f72-4dca-9f2c-c6fc4d2908ee), the usual objection that [the warm brain decoheres far too fast](record:f99f1a35-d100-42a5-b2b8-1e54d357ce9b) does not reach this mechanism, which [even depends on decoherence](record:b75191b6-9fc4-4131-89ad-dedbc6f571cd).

The picture carries further claims: [the physical world is information](record:37db5f10-cb0d-4022-9e2c-351d91530f5b) carried by the quantum state, [reality unfolds through a preferred sequence of nows](record:19453687-cacb-4c37-8721-c95576c2d6de), choice is real and [part of causation without being random](record:26335682-027c-4da0-b823-655bafba982a), and [a quantum self-image restores a ground for values](record:19094f1b-8b48-4c42-954a-c403be22a34b). Stapp offers [binocular rivalry](record:9945ff85-2af1-4986-982d-dedd6436e1e1) and [self-directed neuroplasticity](record:4dd56622-17b5-4d54-9a1c-c1d0addc3e7d) as fits, and grants that no experiment has yet separated his quantum brain from a classical one.
