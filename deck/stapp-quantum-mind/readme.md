# Quantum Interactive Dualism

Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted, unreviewed. Not written or endorsed by Henry P. Stapp. To propose a correction, open an issue or pull request that changes the record and cites the source.

This is a [form.surf](https://form.surf) model repository for the quantum interactive dualism of Henry P. Stapp, a theoretical physicist at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory: orthodox quantum theory in von Neumann's form leaves open which question is put to nature, mind fills that opening, and the quantum Zeno effect lets repeated attention hold a brain state in place. Each claim is one JSON record in `proposition/`, with its kind (definitional, empirical, mathematical, philosophical, normative), its epistemic status, and the source and section it was taken from. The quantum formalism Stapp uses is marked mathematical, his reading of it philosophical, and his claims about attention, effort and the brain empirical. Findings he cites are in `observation/`, each pointing at the paper that reports them, and `link/` joins claims to each other and to evidence. The objections Stapp answers, chief among them decoherence, are kept as claims with `voice: "critique"`.

Every record has `provenance.ai_written: true` and `reviewed: false` until a named person checks it against its sources.

Format: form.surf/1. Check with `form check .`.
