# Integrated Information Theory, in plain words

Integrated information theory (IIT), developed by Giulio Tononi with
Christof Koch and colleagues, starts where it says every theory of
consciousness must start: from experience itself. That [experience exists](record:46c7d927-a1f0-4b92-99d4-33ac02902824)
cannot be doubted, since the doubt is itself an experience. IIT then names
five properties it holds are true of every experience: each one exists
for the one who has it ([intrinsic](record:0a7d29f3-2682-4674-99c7-3d5216b99124)), is this
particular experience ([specific](record:929bab13-53ea-4031-b329-f863af8bf046)), is one whole
([unitary](record:3a867c0a-4c2d-43f5-9218-befb2227feae)), has definite borders ([definite](record:506ee0b7-a98c-41e9-9274-d4a329c974b3)),
and is made of distinctions and relations ([structured](record:0cf80ed8-5c76-437e-8d73-71a1bb218db3)).
These are the axioms.

The theory next asks what a physical system must be like to account for
those properties. In IIT, to exist physically is to have [cause-effect power](record:583e795b-44e3-4581-8f4a-96c55e60ea0f),
the power to take and make a difference. Each axiom becomes a postulate
about that power: it must act within the system itself, select one
state, be [irreducible to its parts](record:5e7bdd89-3f68-4664-8201-fca0e796541e), and be the one set of
units for which this irreducibility, measured as [integrated information](record:eda54694-c154-404f-a104-b22636b8e360)
or phi, is greatest. That set is a [complex](record:be66d721-0340-41bc-9161-efc2d585e132). The central
claim is an [identity](record:79c54001-d62b-4535-9bf1-5ca0a2766a35): an experience is the full
cause-effect structure unfolded from a complex, its quality is the shape
of that structure and its quantity is how irreducible the structure is.

Several consequences follow that the originators accept openly.
Consciousness is a [fundamental property](record:341c2d11-7ee3-4f23-a594-602e26a90f85), graded and
[widespread](record:45569ebd-c53e-431d-bb52-ef02ca1c9a74). A [feed-forward network](record:a50ae510-6e08-4e03-9c17-1674edf0b434)
has no integrated information, so a [digital computer](record:15b9bbf0-af3e-4dc6-8e7b-afbab57dceb6)
that behaves exactly like a person would experience next to nothing. In the
brain, the theory points to [posterior cortex](record:fe02a59f-4a93-4f35-9d54-34725cd7dff6)
rather than the [cerebellum](record:fe84cdaf-4167-4497-8f6a-b43309d0c77f), explains why
consciousness [fades in dreamless sleep](record:4b140503-af00-4104-982a-a6a0a54d3532), and inspired the
[perturbational complexity index](record:54bb8e6a-aef1-4018-98af-7f25828cb702) used with unresponsive
patients. It predicts that experienced space comes from [grid-like](record:81b3a047-3ca5-4a22-a2aa-c1d8e961f0ae)
cortex and that changing connections can change experience even
[without changing activity](record:7b7234a4-d7b1-428d-a844-307b2bb85f39).

The theory is contested. In a preregistered adversarial test published in
2025, one of IIT's agreed predictions, [sustained synchronization](record:09acad59-7163-4633-b357-ed609e4b3567)
within posterior cortex, was not found, while others were partly
supported. A 2023 open letter called the theory [pseudoscience](record:bdc50fe4-f024-4141-bfdc-e266c86a3b32),
and others argue that its [axioms](record:0fa8261a-f75d-49a7-9764-893ace04a5d3) do not do the
work required, or that structure-based theories are [untestable](record:d1f75ebe-f126-4131-b8f5-2e21b0ee7f3a).
The originators [reply](record:fb57a879-def1-4c3f-9049-3233783225e2) that the dispute is
between a consciousness-first paradigm and computational functionalism.
