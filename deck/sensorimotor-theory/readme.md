# Sensorimotor Contingency Theory

Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted, unreviewed. Not written or endorsed by J. Kevin O'Regan and Alva Noë. To propose a correction, open an issue or pull request that changes the record and cites the source.

This is a [form.surf](https://form.surf) model repository for the sensorimotor contingency theory of the psychologist J. Kevin O'Regan and the philosopher Alva Noë: seeing is a way of acting, the experience of seeing occurs when an organism exercises its mastery of the laws by which sensory stimulation changes with movement, and the outside world serves as its own external memory instead of being copied into a detailed internal picture. Each claim is one JSON record in `proposition/`, with its kind (definitional, empirical, philosophical, normative), its epistemic status, and the source and page it was taken from. The empirical claims are about change blindness, sensory substitution and cortical plasticity, and are marked observed where the papers report the results. The claims about qualia and the explanatory gap are philosophical. The main published objections, by Ned Block, Andy Clark, Jesse Prinz and the commentators on the 2001 target article, are kept as claims with `voice: "critique"`.

Every record has `provenance.ai_written: true` and `reviewed: false` until a named person checks it against its sources.

Format: form.surf/1. Check with `form check .`.
