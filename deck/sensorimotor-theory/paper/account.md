# Sensorimotor Contingency Theory

The theory starts from a puzzle about the brain. Finding a cortical map or a pattern of oscillation that goes with seeing would not explain why it feels like seeing, or why seeing differs from hearing. O'Regan and Noë change the question. [Seeing is a way of acting](record:e8c9d52a-cc6f-4c8b-a7b9-de976a38af05), a mode of [exploration mediated by knowledge](record:6f659605-978a-482e-87f0-6d41b8624e99) of [sensorimotor contingencies](record:22824dc0-f658-4713-ba74-748beb69f5de), the laws by which sensory input changes when the eyes, head or body move or when objects move.

Two kinds of law matter. Some are fixed by the visual apparatus, such as the way a straight line distorts on the curved retina as the eye moves, and [ground visual sensation](record:f75c9e6e-246a-4c8b-8355-26d6ee89ae64). Others are fixed by the attributes of objects. [The senses differ by their laws](record:16744364-70d5-4eff-bc01-cedabb0ffe55), not by the nerves that carry them. The knowledge is [practical, not propositional](record:7a863cef-ae6a-4c28-956e-73a40a2ca6f4), and it [must be exercised now](record:4b671793-30ed-4ae2-af9a-d6c98a682a92). A perceiver becomes [aware of a feature](record:ad6b2bde-17e8-4e6d-b34b-36c6ed775858) when this exercise is put to use in thought, planning and speech, and [awareness comes in degrees](record:b17e4b06-b438-4f63-a484-67e2c6df009c).

O'Regan's earlier paper proposed that [the world is an outside memory](record:a0635d26-f2d8-4806-8316-ba4b57825f6c), probed at will, so that [the felt richness of the scene is its immediate availability](record:90527939-edab-4383-9215-62facc05866e) and [there is no detailed picture inside](record:b23f1f3f-1338-475e-8c5a-22797da92712). That idea prompted the change blindness experiments, in which [large changes in full view go unseen](record:fac9c792-8b3d-43bf-a522-993693d853f9) and [observers can look straight at a change and miss it](record:1f4b2527-0e9e-4e8e-a48f-ae49cbde9835). Tactile sensory substitution gave a second test: [objects were identified only when the user controlled the camera](record:a6e27ff0-100f-4269-84d1-c847bc3010fe).

On experience, the authors say [qualia are a category mistake](record:d9613503-4d23-4407-94f7-a9d716f0990e), since experiences are things done, not states, while [experience still has qualitative character](record:72389c9a-7317-4cac-8ce6-23dd44ff6848), as [driving a Porsche](record:ef14167a-1eee-45c2-9153-cba9d0ae2f02) shows. So [there is no explanatory gap](record:b4b880f9-b917-4fb0-83c0-d8161ea0c121). In the reply to commentators they add [bodiliness](record:81824012-4c3c-4893-9854-1dfe822d9b4f) and [grabbiness](record:8553d6a0-fec3-4ebc-a95a-0c109a18d582), which [mark perception off from thought](record:7448c7ea-34d1-4a3d-8101-50cf1c93dab1). Hurley and Noë narrow the target to [the comparative gaps](record:b5aa0b5a-40f6-4701-8c4c-062a03ba162e), using [cortical deference](record:8d10af60-1b70-4b52-a832-4b70d91c54b5) in [rewired ferrets](record:ea93ee72-69f8-48b0-9ae4-d2313816ec2e) and [blind Braille readers](record:c161e215-3560-4043-8018-61b4d6930b34). Noë's book states the same view as [the enactive approach](record:f0259a64-d1b1-481d-b814-402d566f4e0e), and O'Regan's later work extends it to [the locus](record:b62b8e96-384a-4468-8470-1eb3450eab4b) and [presence](record:d50cb30c-3c51-4bec-ab58-1e1a6452123a) of experience.

The objections are that the view is [a kind of behaviorism](record:cb3eb7f3-03df-4514-8c36-c32165d7ec58) refuted by [the paralyzed perceiver and the laptop](record:2412279d-585f-4c11-a4da-1eab27e1bacd), that [the hard problem remains](record:2fe54859-8f1d-4005-9ace-a05292feab21), that the sensorimotor part may be [causal rather than constitutive](record:57f1f1f5-8aa5-4ca7-a356-c30bb7c5c827), that [motor deficits leave perception intact](record:c7250e3a-7780-47dc-a7ac-dd17b6988285), and that [its key terms are unclear](record:da938313-c032-412d-b2e8-e1cb8bd4c7f8).
