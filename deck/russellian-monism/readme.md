# Russellian Monism

Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted, unreviewed. Not written or endorsed by Bertrand Russell, Arthur Eddington, David J. Chalmers, Torin Alter, Yujin Nagasawa, Derk Pereboom and Philip Goff. To propose a correction, open an issue or pull request that changes the record and cites the source.

This is a [form.surf](https://form.surf) model repository for Russellian monism, the family of views on which a single set of properties underlies both consciousness and the most basic entities of physics. Its roots are Bertrand Russell's The Analysis of Matter (1927) and Arthur Eddington's The Nature of the Physical World (1928). Its modern statements are David J. Chalmers's type-F monism and his panpsychism and panprotopsychism, Derk Pereboom's physicalist version with absolutely intrinsic properties, the overviews by Torin Alter and Sam Coleman and by Philip Goff and Sam Coleman, and Goff's intelligible emergentist version. Each claim is one JSON record in `proposition/`, with its kind (definitional, empirical, mathematical, philosophical), its epistemic status, and the source and locator it was taken from. The view is philosophical: no claim here is tested by experiment, theses are marked hypothesized, what the authors derive from them inferred, and definitions designed. Objections the sources state, among them the combination problem, Howell's mental causation argument and Goff's case against constitutive forms, are kept as claims with `voice: "critique"`, with replies where the sources give them.

Every record has `provenance.ai_written: true` and `reviewed: false` until a named person checks it against its sources.

Format: form.surf/1. Check with `form check .`.
