# Qualia Formalism

**Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted, unreviewed, and not written or endorsed by the Qualia Research Institute, Andrés Gómez-Emilsson or Michael Edward Johnson.** To propose a correction, open an issue or a pull request that changes the record and cites the source passage.

A form.surf model repository: the claims of the Qualia Research Institute (QRI), stated as Andrés Gómez-Emilsson, Michael Edward Johnson and Chris Percy state them in their papers and essays. The symmetry theory of valence is its testable center, and field topology is its answer to the binding and boundary problems.

## What is here

- `proposition/`: the framework's claims, one per file: qualia formalism and the symmetry theory of valence, the neural proxies (harmonics, compressibility, annealing), psychedelic phenomenology, field-topology binding, and heavy-tailed valence. Published criticisms are `voice: "critique"`. Answers to the nine form.surf questions start with "Answer:".
- `observation/`: the evidence the framework cites and its limits.
- `link/`: dependencies (`requires`, `assumes`), evidence (`supports`, `weakens`), and two places where QRI's own sources disagree (`is_in_tension_with`).
- `source/`: every paper and essay cited, each opened when this was written. QRI essays are `web_page`.
- `paper/account.md`: the framework in plain words.

## How to read the labels

- **epistemic_status**: how the claim was reached. Nearly every QRI claim is hypothesized, and the evidence records say how thin the tests so far are.
- **standing**: left empty. This repository records claims, it does not rule on them.
- **provenance.ai_written: true, reviewed: false** on every record: a model extracted it and no person has yet checked it against its source.

## Corrections

Every record cites its source with a locator and, where checked, a short verbatim quote. If a record misstates the framework, open an issue or pull request with the passage that shows it. Format: form.surf/1, specified at https://form.surf/guides/developers/format.
