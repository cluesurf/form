Many theories say a system is conscious because of something it does over time: it broadcasts information, loops signals back, updates predictions, or runs a program. Aneil Mallavarapu and Chris Percy argue that no such theory can be kept alongside two commitments science already makes. The first is that [the same present state with the same inputs has the same future](record:2a196211-3758-4f49-84f7-54dea848daa4), which is why an experiment can be repeated. The second is that [consciousness makes some difference](record:c4ba1be2-3225-439d-b445-92fc589d078a) to what a system does, or no test could find it.

Their example is a [gear computer that runs a 1000-step program](record:2bbcd9a3-eddc-40d7-bc35-e965cd139f7a) said to make it conscious, and a second machine assembled straight into the first one's state without running it. From then on the two turn alike forever, and the second even holds the first one's memories, since memory is in the gears. A theory that calls only the first conscious names a difference that either shows up in behavior, which breaks the first commitment, or never shows up, which breaks the second. Written as three premises, [history matters](record:9e802fef-93f7-408e-ae4b-73f459ded021), same state and inputs give the same future, and consciousness makes a difference, [the three cannot all hold](record:fc56b2aa-9563-4e6e-b343-2a06ce82191b). Keeping the last two, [consciousness depends only on the present state](record:7b064350-7eaa-4ca3-a5e2-dbbe49d178f3), $C_t = f(X_t)$: ["Consciousness is due not to what a system has done, but what it is."](record:9ecfecae-0f65-4348-a44f-5eb40931b4ee) The whole universe may still hold traces of how the system got there, but [the theory assigns consciousness to the system](record:e7e688bc-f1a8-4783-ba23-7d5f10bb6e32), and inside it the two machines are the same. The argument [carries over to random dynamics](record:440545ae-ff02-469e-8449-a2ce19fd5118) with probabilities in place of exact futures, and [an exact copy only has to be possible](record:cd355d71-9c4a-4360-b9b6-5c3d27159ad4), though [for a computer it can be built](record:d5638976-781f-4dff-bc12-a640a314100a) by setting its bits.

This does not make history unimportant: [learning and growth are how a brain reaches its present state, and its memories live in that state](record:22f9fc08-5255-45a6-901d-21d26cb0167f). What it rules out is having gone through a process counting again once the state is fixed. So [process theories must restate themselves as claims about the present state](record:52c95bcc-3664-4a27-9bd0-1618fbc959ef). IIT [must derive its units, boundary and time scale from the present state](record:626a1bd3-4120-45e8-bcfc-23e6aa73b2f1), which today [are chosen by the analyst](record:1ff53adb-66af-4ab8-9a3e-1189fa3ed183). Consciousness in a digital computer [needs a physical property that chips, vacuum tubes and gears running one program all share](record:d9bef6a0-567c-4551-8396-d17b6db4d4cd). [Field theories and some quantum theories already fit](record:03f83c7c-0e31-45fd-a108-9409b614ce40), though [the theorem favors none of them](record:195fa85b-6fd3-4707-b0e5-773d6306fd64).

The paper names its own limits. [Systems without a complete local state of their own](record:19f55471-537f-401d-8280-0aa4606c9807), such as entangled parts, are outside its scope, and [the theory under test chooses where the conscious system ends](record:137a8c61-2dc2-4eed-aec2-95639b5b6a8c). Its last sentence goes further than the proof, as a perspective rather than a result: [perhaps mind is a state of matter, and matter is a state of mind](record:88716908-d94d-4bfe-9f62-0e110474aa15).
