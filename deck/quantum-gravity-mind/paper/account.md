Paola Zizzi extends John Wheeler's "it from bit" to [it from qubit](record:00a45ff6-c617-4648-97b2-1e6c750c04f3): a theory of quantum gravity can be written in terms of qubits stored in space at the Planck scale. In loop quantum gravity, edges of spin networks pierce a surface and give it area, and Zizzi reads an edge in the spin one half state as a qubit, so [each pixel of Planck area holds one qubit](record:b2465416-73c6-45a9-a345-6bec100d2178). Discrete, qubitized space-time is then a quantum memory register processed by unitary gates that belong to space-time itself: [quantum space-time is a quantum computer](record:ea300a29-9f71-44f3-8d04-690fcde16d32). Its evolution [is reversible](record:d90773f3-3b2a-45e8-8b20-62f5bd92fe0c), it is [entangled and non-local at the Planck scale](record:edb750ec-99e9-4b6e-bae2-f1ca547dafe5), and the functions it computes [are the laws of physics](record:51554d7b-cb8c-4f87-b599-3cdd84621d59). Later papers give the entanglement a geometry, [a tiny wormhole between Planck pixels on successive time slices](record:ce81d861-4e70-4469-bc7b-551a2e37588a).

The cosmology follows. The early universe is [a quantum register that grows with time](record:be9e9226-a67e-4504-8951-b74922993759), its qubits the pixels of the de Sitter horizon, and vacuum fluctuations [speed the growth, which is inflation](record:c54b49fb-26d6-45c3-bce4-0ed20808ffd9), while the network [saturates the physical bounds on computation](record:570f6306-0c0f-4603-a9f2-3b4587133463). During inflation the universe is a superposition of registers, and by Penrose's objective reduction it [self-reduces at the end of inflation](record:9b7ac78c-65f1-41f8-a639-a5d3129219bc), which [accounts for the low entropy of the universe](record:9d09eabf-b1f4-4000-91cf-eed6cca6f5e8).

The consciousness claim rests on a number. About $10^9$ registers reduce at the end of inflation, [the same number as the tubulins in one Orch OR event](record:398eb289-cfca-4e3d-b000-13f23139dcfb). With Chalmers' principle that [the same organization gives the same experience](record:18b99ccc-557a-4bfb-9aeb-6c999bc05a12), Zizzi conjectures that [the early universe had a conscious experience](record:87fa1a76-7a33-4a7a-ba54-cb032c89e920), and that [human minds carry an imprint of it](record:dac3b2f2-d450-40ce-add3-6e0bbd591907), a point she calls [very speculative](record:e6c66621-ff88-444b-a911-c2a8ffdb3a0d). Her later work on the mind distinguishes [a fast quantum mode of thought and a conscious classical mode](record:6b7fa5ef-b0e5-41c5-b3da-efa043c148fe), whose [flashes of decoherence feel like a flow](record:68c093a1-9032-47a2-9862-6a176ae37e65), from [metathought, which is non-algorithmic](record:d2ed3d32-acc8-4e64-8661-1001ca249bbf) and [separates humans from machines](record:08eb3f0b-8d6f-4a4e-a0c0-646042082273). Against Tegmark's [estimate that tubulin superpositions decohere in under a picosecond](record:069e8703-7827-4c8b-aeec-a2a3cc16bfb3), she proposes that [a dissipative gauge theory of the brain protects the qubits](record:23035957-bac7-4b3d-a72a-15f9c8a65990).
