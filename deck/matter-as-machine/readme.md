# Matter As Machine

**Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted (claude-opus-5-5), unreviewed. Not written or endorsed by the author of matterasmachine.com.**
To propose a correction, open an issue or a pull request that changes the record in question, citing the page and locator that shows the error. A correction from the author of matterasmachine.com is especially welcome.

Matter As Machine, from [matterasmachine.com](https://matterasmachine.com/): everything is execution. Particles are cyclic machines, looped lists of unit steps in six directions, and the same algorithmic view casts evolution as slow creation by simple machines, steered by instincts such as the instinct to change the world. The site gives the author no personal name, so this folder names the originator as the author of matterasmachine.com.

This is a form.surf model repository (format `form.surf/1`). It holds the site's own claims, each as one JSON record in `proposition/`, the studies and simulations the site reports in `observation/`, and the relations between them in `link/`. Every record names its sources in `provenance.sources`, with a locator (the page's section) and a short verbatim quote. `paper/account.md` is the plain account.

## Sources and rights

- The sources are 19 pages of matterasmachine.com saved on 2026-10-19, each a `web_page` source with its original URL. Only the pages the site marks free to read are used. The two pages for supporters only are not.
- The site states no open license, so every source keeps its own copyright: `license` is null and `hostable` false. The records restate the ideas in new words, with quotes of one or two sentences.
- The folder's own records are CC-BY-4.0.

## Labels

- `provenance.ai_written: true`: a model extracted the record from the cited page.
- `provenance.reviewed: false`: no person has yet read the record against its page.
- `standing`: null on every claim. The three predictions (slow photons, running wheels for crowded colonies, mistaken eureka moments) carry a `prediction` with no outcome, since no page reports one.
- Observations record studies as the site reports them, with the site's page as the source. The studies themselves were not opened for these records.
- No `critique` records yet: this folder was built from the site's own pages only.
- Questionnaire: questions 3 (experience) and 6 (self) are open, since the pages do not answer them.
