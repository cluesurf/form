Matter As Machine starts from one question: [what if everything is execution](record:134f6026-8a9d-4601-83d5-1ab101d3de1d), with matter following a few simple, discrete rules whose consequences reach from physics to evolution and civilization? Execution here means a system carrying out its rules, nothing more. Computation is narrower, execution with a goal that someone sets, so [a universe with no goal is execution, not computation](record:31ae8955-a4a2-4823-941b-94df711441a4). And [if matter executes an algorithm, people, being made of matter, execute algorithms too](record:8133da9c-4662-4704-ba2e-59a8a1037c6d). The site is careful that [these reasons are not evidence](record:d2006504-0ab7-47aa-b9ad-2013a35bda10): the model stands or falls on its predictions and tests.

In the physics, [each particle is a cyclic machine](record:5cb1a80c-e9cb-4470-a2c1-a146e2e6aa4b): a looped list of instructions, each one a unit step left, right, up, down, forward or back, one step per tick of absolute time. Familiar quantities become counts. [Energy is the number of states](record:4d580562-87f0-4af1-a420-4519a0b1fc27), each worth $\hbar$. [Inertia is the machine running on until its states are updated](record:92acd99d-4ed4-40c3-8cbe-54128acef09c), and more states resist change more. [Speed is the share of net steps in the loop](record:5ae97929-0ae1-4f98-a28f-9d5d1f716899), so RRRRLLL moves at $1/7$ of the maximum, and [rest mass is the states that only loop](record:21034d30-2162-4a77-871a-7f6433e7f8a3), like a left-right pair that goes nowhere but takes time. [Interaction destroys the original particle](record:bf9842d7-7f42-4748-a911-409d4645a878), which is where uncertainty comes from. A second rule adds [reset events](record:289ebd96-3b2e-427e-9ad2-aae40d96fd10): every quantum leaves its particle once per Hubble time, $1/H_0$, and travels alone until it joins another. The third part, [the rules by which particles exchange quanta, is not yet found](record:8ff3e565-4305-4cb7-ae79-48ae7fdc1eae).

The physics prediction is slow photons. If [light separates from its source at $c - v$ in absolute time](record:9c6f12fc-c376-4caf-b945-5748db065dea), then for the source to measure its own light at $c$ [its clock must tick slower by $(c - v)/c$](record:e0fb528c-08d7-44ec-b7d3-d442b86bce2d), and [light reflected from a surface moving away from the detector travels at about $c - 2v$](record:d72c4940-93b0-4904-b50f-24fe5e9adb15). The proposed test times light bounced off the edge of a rotating disk against a direct pulse, at rest and at several speeds, looking for an extra delay. No result is reported yet.

The other half applies the same view to life. [Evolution is creation, only slow](record:055e15e8-490c-47f3-9843-01ca22b7c490): the construction of the world by simple machines with no plan, and [what evolves is algorithms](record:6d58638d-d4c1-4752-aed7-9ec171e7c436). [Instincts set its direction](record:2f538a3f-7cfc-4bbd-9f68-627018eb8916), since behavior decides which traits are favored, and [depression may be a direct form of self-selection](record:664a1500-a24a-4133-9731-f8354668b210), a shutdown when an instinctive need goes unmet, testable by giving [crowded mouse colonies running wheels](record:a9d3ecc7-d871-4b04-a3ae-66399a43fc30). In people the key drive is [an instinct to change the world their way](record:12a02a23-7a7f-4a98-b340-cbe7d4d1b99e), steered by eureka moments that [assign importance to ideas, not truth](record:67c4b7b1-3812-477f-93db-e01c28cfae39), so that [a mistaken insight should feel as convincing as a sound one](record:c6ff8d75-f78e-4754-97e4-316b8e4bff9a).
