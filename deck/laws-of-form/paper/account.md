Louis Kauffman builds on George Spencer-Brown's Laws of Form, a calculus that starts from nothing but [a distinction, a space divided into a marked and an unmarked side](record:35a9a961-16c5-484a-84e3-e71cb918449c). The mark is [both the name of a value and the operation of crossing the boundary](record:1de3ce9b-91ea-4262-bd38-320f0eda031d), and since it makes a distinction in its own space, it refers to itself. Spencer-Brown also proposed imaginary logical values, by analogy with the square root of minus one. Kauffman and Arthur Collings make these precise in the BF calculus, where [imaginary values act as cyclic operators, like a clock marked at the quarter hours](record:84b8667d-9a63-482b-9307-f6786e4c1329), and which is [an axiomatically complete extension of Laws of Form](record:13d5ec2e-774e-4dde-b758-35878c6faf8f). A 16-valued version, the Q-Calculus, [yields the non-commutative quaternion group from marks](record:0c5613fd-6e04-4ce1-a7f9-f693dc9719ac).

The same idea runs through his algebra. The square root of minus one is [an oscillation between plus and minus one](record:0bb6c936-bfcf-4b9a-b57a-cb7712251b1c), and [an iterant, a periodic waveform seen from one starting point or the other](record:4917eecb-ff8e-4476-a005-f570b45ddeda), turns that oscillation into an algebra that [contains all of matrix algebra](record:201cd9f5-f423-41e9-bca8-2bad68c28f89). A basic construction [produces Clifford algebras step by step](record:1c82a732-fd35-45c4-9927-ec67d4461efe), and the simplest of them combined with itself is [the structure underlying Majorana fermions and all fermions](record:947116ed-7928-43b5-9ff1-0a874cf8e57d). With Pierre Noyes he solved the discrete Dirac equation exactly as [a sum over lattice paths, Feynman's checkerboard](record:99cb3f20-5255-4dda-93bf-9eb8cecc478a).

In his noncommutative worlds, [a commutator plays the role of a derivative](record:1b9840d3-789c-49c2-b67f-3213b6cf34ef), with no continuum, no spaces and no limits, and [Hamilton's equations, the Levi-Civita connection and gauge fields](record:e000f324-a5ee-4a69-9018-bf5890cb7f0e) appear from the algebra. Because a discrete measurement takes a tick of the clock, observing a value and its change in two orders differ, and that difference is [the diffusion constant of a Brownian walk](record:b167ef5c-3de8-4cde-91e4-397fbc20345f), with no probability assumed. With collaborators he argues that [neither the Schrödinger equation nor the wave function is fundamental](record:6fc37faa-8205-49e7-8877-ea54c021c0f7), that [entanglement and the connectivity of space are intimately related](record:a2d72b35-d58c-4084-ab72-f7ba7660dd7f), that in a preon model [braids are chirality and twists are charge](record:5f29e68c-a8bc-419b-bdb1-50fc31041f4a), and that the Mereon geometry is [the three-dimensional shadow of the 600-cell](record:0855bc84-ae33-4e1b-9970-d53a5342fd7c). He notes that [physics on a noncommutative space with topology remains to be done](record:4af3ba96-5d0e-4d0e-b275-5acc9c1a9c07), and the preon model [reaches only part of the standard model](record:7b7e19f0-7ac2-4520-b5a9-08b88cb1add5).
