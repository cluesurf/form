# Patterns

What two or more models of the form repository have in common or at
issue. Format: form.surf/1, specified at
https://form.surf/guides/developers/format.

A pattern is never a claim of its own. It points at the models' claims,
which stay in their own folders with their own evidence and standing.

- **Same idea**: `proposition/`. One idea that several models hold, in a
  neutral wording of its own so no model's words are favored. Its
  `origin` names each model's own record of it.
- **Contradiction** and **tension**: `link/`, of relation `contradicts`
  or `is_in_tension_with`, whose two ends sit in different models.
- **Evidence across models**: a `supports` or `weakens` link from an
  observation in one model to a claim in another.
- **Narrower and broader**: `specializes` or `generalizes` between models.

The fifteen questions every model answers are in `question/`, and each same
idea that answers one links to it with `answers`.

A pattern carries no standing. Each model keeps its own verdict on its
own claim, and agreement among models is a lead, never proof. Every
record here is AI-written and unreviewed, and nothing here is endorsed by
the originators of any framework.

## Correcting it

Suggest a pattern, a correction to one, or its removal as a pull request
whose diff includes a `proposal/` record. The curators of this folder
merge it, every decision public with its reasoning. A change to a model's
own claims is a pull request against that model's folder.
