# Computational Functionalism

[Functionalism](record:28452a77-05b7-4d56-932b-97420db1ba13) holds that what makes a state a pain or a belief is its role in the system, not its internal constitution, so [mental states can be multiply realized](record:553bb04f-8a04-4886-839e-d040d4061dc3). Putnam's [machine state functionalism](record:91172323-cb38-4591-be21-6167d1ec19cd) modeled a mind as a probabilistic automaton whose mental states are [machine table states](record:e013cf56-e67e-46e1-8fca-f203f2c59748). [Computational functionalism](record:b15e76c5-6d8a-435b-a0da-2ac71788aa5b) says the relevant organization is computational: a state is conscious when it plays the right role in [an algorithm](record:aa8e74f6-61cb-4651-a168-49e06f9c018c), so [the substrate matters only through the algorithms it can run](record:580d3e86-382e-4a65-acaf-99bfc5515747), though [not every substrate will do](record:d1dc4af4-e953-47d4-a6bc-d6bc14bbb08c) and [the same input-output function is not enough](record:dd8e597b-26cd-4efe-b8e5-83ee06f00091).

The 2023 report [adopts the thesis as a working hypothesis](record:d5d796af-9c1f-4561-a221-4d98a76a69cd) because [most scientific theories of consciousness can be read computationally](record:43211f29-7aed-4c77-bb1f-2cad8c707697). It argues for [a theory-heavy approach](record:3740e65c-27a3-4ad1-ba0a-4ac73673ec4d), since [behavioral tests can be gamed](record:907cdba1-d107-4987-8d06-ab3fd65da47a), and derives [indicator properties](record:0b1eaba0-86e1-4d02-bcdd-57666e93c42e) from recurrent processing, global workspace, higher-order, attention schema and predictive processing theories, [setting IIT aside](record:6466ea6c-db38-4eb7-b128-e8895c6703cb). Its finding is that [no current AI system is a strong candidate, with no obvious technical barrier to building one that meets the indicators](record:85fd3973-291f-4f18-a121-43167b8cce52). Chalmers holds that [silicon is as apt as carbon](record:72e12562-6c63-4409-998c-b475c232d20d), gives [current language models a low credence](record:d329473f-e66b-4122-a9ad-6501be7a85a6), and expects [serious candidates within a decade](record:5c8690a9-da9a-4256-acc7-67a2a6630a40). Chalmers's [fading qualia argument](record:c49da628-ecfb-4a5a-91fb-3a45232638d0) supports the thesis.

Critics answer with [the Chinese room](record:a9e10ade-0990-404c-9fa7-73bd0849722e), [simulation is not duplication](record:6ab9db8d-3c17-4c05-8f45-a39c155267de), [absent qualia in the China brain](record:567fdb6d-f1c9-46c7-baef-049375a3fa2b), [inverted qualia](record:18d65f81-ac43-4432-bc0b-d330bc017495), and the view that [consciousness needs life or a material](record:cbe60d09-330e-4012-a264-f0ba607cc1fb). The Churchlands reply that [intrinsic properties are no problem for functionalism](record:2a5bd14f-39ad-4101-af21-1f379b5d73b1), that [the China brain is far too small](record:f51cbb93-d94e-47b9-bc76-dd16ab096472), and that [no mental state has intrinsic intentionality](record:e915259c-7b62-4b03-b679-04454a8d8d88).
