**Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted (claude-opus-5-5), unreviewed. Not written or endorsed by Victor Logvinovich and Jean-Claude Perez.**
To propose a correction, open an issue or a pull request that changes the record in question, citing the source and locator that shows the error. A correction from Victor Logvinovich or Jean-Claude Perez is especially welcome.

Victor Logvinovich's IT3 framework (from April 2026) and its IT3-WavePixel extension (October 2026), several papers written with Jean-Claude Perez. The vacuum is modeled as a compact constructible manifold over the multiquadratic field $K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3}, \sqrt{5})$, which the papers derive from a Pythagorean cascade that stops at $\sqrt{13}$. From it they read a closure of the periodic table at $Z = 172$ as an FCC crystal of 43 cells, a mass gap that is automatic on the compact manifold, gravity as the surface tension of the vacuum crystal with no graviton, and the elements as a binary register in a deterministic finite-state machine.

The framework makes claims about physical reality only. It takes no position on experience, consciousness, selves, intelligence, life or agency, and the questionnaire answers say so. The work is deposited on Zenodo under CC BY 4.0 and announced on X. It is not peer reviewed.

This is a form.surf model repository (format `form.surf/1`). It holds the framework's own claims, each as one JSON record in `proposition/`, and the relations between them in `link/`. Every record names its sources in `provenance.sources`, with a locator and, where it could be checked, a short verbatim quote. `paper/account.md` is the plain account.

## Labels

- `provenance.ai_written: true`: a model extracted the record from the cited source.
- `provenance.reviewed: false`: no person has yet read the record against its source.
- `epistemic_status: "inferred"` marks a statement the papers prove as pure mathematics. Every physical reading of it is `hypothesized`.
- `prediction`: the falsifiers the capacity paper names, the 1088 GeV state at the HL-LHC among them. Every `outcome` is null.
- There are no `observation/` records and no `voice: "critique"` claims: no source reports a test of an IT3 prediction, and no published criticism was found.
