# IT3, the plain account

Victor Logvinovich treats the vacuum as a crystal rather than a continuum. Its metric is generated from the number 1 by a chain of right triangles: $1^2 + (\sqrt{2})^2 = (\sqrt{3})^2$, then $(\sqrt{2})^2 + (\sqrt{3})^2 = (\sqrt{5})^2$. The numbers reached form the field $K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3}, \sqrt{5})$ ([the cascade](record:0b2ea491-665d-4039-93b5-2b0e9ba4f970)), and the next step would need $\sqrt{13}$, which is not in $K$. The capacity paper calls this the Kummer wall and holds that it forces space to close up and cuts off the ultraviolet catastrophe ([the wall](record:7a9e6d6c-7da2-464f-9bd2-8c731d44ca6a)). The Yang-Mills paper, written twelve days earlier, treats the same link as an open conjecture and calls the cascade a structural resonance, not a derivation ([the hedge](record:42df6eee-5946-4b85-8968-cfcbf164bc44)).

On the compact manifold $S^4 \times T^3$ built over $K$, the papers find that a mass gap follows automatically, while on the open space $\mathbb{R}^4$ of the Clay problem the gap and the required axioms cannot both hold ([mass gap](record:1a8d2363-7dca-4176-b966-17f0330a8d9f)). The same geometry gives the periodic table its shape. A topological index fixes 86 states in each of two mirrored halves, and the 172 states close as exactly 43 face-centered cubic cells, so the table ends at $Z = 172$ ([closure](record:ce0eb81f-c754-4281-b0ca-25082cd720bc)). Rest mass is light confined in the vacuum, and gravity is not a force carried by a graviton but the surface tension of the crystal, which merges defects to reduce its boundary area and so yields the inverse-square law ([gravity](record:b95a559f-9a16-46cc-bbfb-6ab059ed706a)).

IT3-WavePixel recasts this as a deterministic finite-state machine with three registers: the nuclear charge as a hardware address, the neutrons as error-correcting padding, and the electrons as a 172-bit occupation mask ([the register](record:e706f705-5fbd-4054-a32d-5e566bc2ab3c)). Nuclear decays and the lead-to-gold conversion seen at CERN become edits of that register. The papers claim zero free parameters and extend the same number theory to the outer solar system, which they read as quantized. They name their falsifiers: superheavy elements 119 and 120, a resonance at 1088 GeV at the High-Luminosity LHC, and the terminal elements 171 and 172 in simulation ([tests](record:d26d787e-ba70-4e71-98ce-4f4726262e3f)). The framework says nothing about mind or experience.
