# Illusionism

Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted, unreviewed. Not written or endorsed by Keith Frankish and Daniel C. Dennett. To propose a correction, open an issue or pull request that changes the record and cites the source.

This is a [form.surf](https://form.surf) model repository for illusionism about phenomenal consciousness, in the form Keith Frankish named and set out in 2016 and Daniel C. Dennett had defended since his 1988 case against qualia: experiences do not have phenomenal properties, introspection misrepresents them as having such properties, and the problem to solve is the illusion problem, why the illusion arises and why it is so powerful. Each claim is one JSON record in `proposition/`, with its kind (definitional, empirical, philosophical), its epistemic status, and the source and page it was taken from. The theses of the view are marked hypothesized, what follows from them inferred, and the terms the view introduces designed. No detailed illusionist theory exists yet in these sources, and the view's own proposed research is marked hypothesized. Opposing views the sources answer, from Eric Schwitzgebel, David Chalmers and others, are kept as claims with `voice: "critique"`.

Every record has `provenance.ai_written: true` and `reviewed: false` until a named person checks it against its sources.

Format: form.surf/1. Check with `form check .`.
