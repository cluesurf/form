Causal emergence is the claim that a higher-level description of a system can capture more of its causation than the full description of its parts. Erik Hoel, with Larissa Albantakis and Giulio Tononi, made this measurable in 2013 with [effective information](record:8f42382b-1b97-40c3-9a79-60a2eb5a9bba): set a system into each of its possible states with equal probability, see what each state leads to, and count in bits how tightly causes pin down effects. A coarse-grained, macro description groups many micro states into one. It has fewer states, which costs bits, but its causes can be more reliable and more specific. When that gain outweighs the cost, [the macro beats the micro](record:2f9ce65f-d9e0-4afd-b7b3-da8fe68aa8c9), even though the macro is completely fixed by the micro, a point Hoel puts as [the macro supervening on the micro while superseding it causally](record:747e80d6-8e82-4e22-986a-2fb0b6e3d007).

The gain comes from two kinds of looseness at the micro level: noise, where one cause spreads over many effects, and [degeneracy](record:fd49fbed-3dd6-4004-9897-03c3bee6cf1c), where many causes converge on one effect. Grouping micro states can cancel both, [the way an error-correcting code beats a noisy channel](record:63ded878-de1e-426b-8256-8bdbba1c9ae2). The flip side is exact: [a micro level with no noise and no common causes, such as a reversible permutation, leaves no room for emergence](record:9f20f404-7c19-4cc9-afec-cf1c14017209). Hoel and collaborators report [informative higher scales in many real networks](record:382c41cb-fe6a-42d6-8d62-ef15cb4d19b4), in gene regulatory networks and in [protein interactomes across the tree of life](record:6ca2447a-908a-4ca6-8939-935f23e816fc), and argue that [the hierarchy of the sciences may track real causal gains](record:993cdb15-0d4a-4ef9-b783-fc98fb4bc556).

In 2025 Hoel replaced the single best scale of the first version with [Causal Emergence 2.0](record:e5b37246-c587-47b1-976d-ceb8107ca3c8). Causation there rests on two [primitives, sufficiency and necessity](record:9c6341da-fc56-42e3-8581-ab287a006c6f), which [over a dozen measures of causation share](record:34764c7d-248a-43ad-b072-a2d9d4054cbc). A path climbs from the micro to the top scale and assigns to each scale what it adds, so [every scale is one slice of a multiscale whole](record:c7b5e11b-c47d-4b79-ab57-24a09a3cd6c4), and [emergent complexity](record:ccc3e37f-31fa-4f48-9973-b2d027b23c6f) measures how widely causation is spread across scales.

Critics argue that the original gain [depends on imposing a separate uniform distribution at each level](record:9ec9ede8-d286-4d59-a04e-4c111f9e7484), that [macro interventions become ambiguous](record:77906a82-4c3b-4f42-80d9-e28540ecd1c2), and that [the needed assumptions are rarely met by data](record:608545dd-a6d5-43bf-b0ee-4d26ef0c0a3e). Hoel answers that [prediction is not causation](record:14e8a957-511d-4bb4-abc6-d84fddacd955), and CE 2.0 drops the reliance on effective information alone.
