# Integrated World Modeling Theory

Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted, unreviewed. Not written or endorsed by Adam Safron. To propose a correction, open an issue or pull request that changes the record and cites the source.

This is a [form.surf](https://form.surf) model repository for the integrated world modeling theory (IWMT) of Adam Safron. IWMT uses the free energy principle and active inference framework to combine integrated information theory (IIT) and global neuronal workspace theory (GNWT): experience is what it is like to be a probabilistic generative model of an embodied agent's sensorium, once that model has coherence in space, time and cause. Each claim is one JSON record in `proposition/`, with its kind (definitional, empirical, philosophical), its epistemic status, and the source and page it was taken from. The neural mechanism (synchronous complexes called self-organizing harmonic modes, alpha and theta rhythms, turbo coding across cortical autoencoders) and the predictions are empirical. The account of the Hard problem, of panpsychism and of agency is philosophical. Findings Safron cites from other laboratories are in `observation/`, each pointing at the paper that reports it, and `link/` joins claims to each other and to evidence. Published objections that Safron names and answers, among them the unfolding argument and the expander graph objection, are kept as claims with `voice: "critique"`.

Every record has `provenance.ai_written: true` and `reviewed: false` until a named person checks it against its sources.

Format: form.surf/1. Check with `form check .`.
