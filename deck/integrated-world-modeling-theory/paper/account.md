# Integrated World Modeling Theory

IWMT starts from three theories at once. The free energy principle says that anything that persists must model the conditions of its own persistence, integrated information theory says what it is for a system to exist for itself, and global neuronal workspace theory says how information is selected and broadcast. Safron [combines them into one framework](record:3d905170-2b08-4cb5-a0cf-87b0a8a7f017) and states its thesis in one line: [consciousness is what it is like to be a process that generates integrated models of system and world with spatial, temporal and causal coherence](record:2f3ec308-18a3-429a-b246-eab69e08b8ad).

The claims come in layers. [Basic phenomenal consciousness is what it is like to be a generative model of an embodied agent's sensorium](record:438b2b49-0adc-47c8-8989-782eb9976d61). [Access and higher order consciousness need that information integrated into a coherent world model](record:c0e6d225-bd12-4d12-81a1-95dbbf42bece), and [both may need counterfactual modeling of the self and its actions](record:ec50d3ac-65a2-4aa0-9425-1967468422d1). Coherence is the hinge: [a world needs things placed in a space, changes that make time, and regularities that make cause](record:f1b11df4-790c-4e9c-85f7-fae6dbe37942), Kant's preconditions for judgment taken as preconditions for experience, and [embodied autonomous selfhood is added as a further precondition](record:117d48f1-f7ff-4835-be7b-9570f3b98304). So [integrated information and global availability are necessary for consciousness but not sufficient](record:ea235529-f6d9-477e-8a4a-343399e163e6): [without meaningful connections to a structured world a system could have any amount of integration and still be nothing that it is like to be](record:e1174786-969a-4641-9bbe-3ec6ce060dfe).

The mechanism is a synchronous complex called a [self-organizing harmonic mode](record:6d33cfcf-4179-4de8-951d-ccc4875854c5). [Conscious ignition is the crossing of a threshold for forming one](record:7cc3ca29-4012-40fa-8f51-d243e4c0fd7d), and [a maximal SOHM is at once a maximum of integrated information and a global workspace](record:ad66259d-ce19-40ec-854b-c6269e4ada80). IWMT reads [cortical hierarchies as folded variational autoencoders](record:514ea4c7-6010-4454-9b87-a35ff8163576) whose bottlenecks [exchange beliefs by loopy message passing, a turbo code](record:d64c2c15-4766-4467-a606-0e9c04999012). [Alpha rhythms in posterior cortex carry basic experience, and their nesting in theta rhythms couples them to frontal and hippocampal systems for access and volition](record:fdb0c8d3-2644-4b88-8b70-6f5c1c544668). This settles the IIT and GNWT dispute over the substrate by splitting it: [posterior cortex generates experience](record:a8be6f80-ca47-40e2-9df3-29c48bd7c623), the posterior hot zone of IIT, while [the frontal lobes are needed to access, report and manipulate it](record:668881bc-499d-480c-8a15-50a1f49501f2).

The same framework recasts IIT. [Systems exist to the degree they generate evidence for themselves](record:a32d2c3a-ef37-4e5d-abb0-94a93b4ee8d7), [integrated information should track free energy minimization](record:01c1d57b-38c0-4428-aa75-e8200260d98f), and [the boundaries of IIT's complexes are Markov blankets](record:ae13ef85-5dd2-4d81-be44-2c0099b78153). Safron proposes [an IIT-Emergence with the exclusion postulate relaxed](record:4d4a7494-1489-4c61-ab95-57d303e05529), and holds that consciousness is what physics feels like from the inside [only where physics supports coherent system and world models](record:584334fe-185c-430c-b467-80af9cd890d4). IWMT is [panprotopsychist but not panpsychist](record:03d2d102-2011-48c6-a8bd-6681de2448c9): [rocks are not conscious](record:b08ce141-e0fd-452f-8e02-b9902fb47bce), and [brainstems on their own probably are not either](record:ca071651-9099-40e6-995d-084832e83ba6).

On agency, [ignition events that update beliefs about intended action make will power a systemic cause](record:21e82526-9a3a-49d6-9198-a7b843ebaca8), and [the free will worth wanting survives findings such as Libet's](record:f2ac3af0-8cb2-41d5-bcf7-5dcd17a79b1d), since [readiness potentials are accumulating motor predictions that conscious intentions shape](record:bcce9b59-8e42-4467-a6d7-d022e6f7a4c9). On the Hard problem, [experience may be what a model that computes joint posteriors over the causes of sensation is like](record:7e45b792-69a6-4f73-a167-90a327c47cc9), and [the problem may be solved through bridging principles rather than dissolved](record:e2e22cd0-08fa-47cc-b055-2df9b34489f6), though Safron [does not claim a definitive solution](record:7fd8fbec-812a-440c-84c0-cd6ed2bed81e). On machines, [current generative models are not conscious](record:705500d9-ecd1-436e-9b89-47bc7ca3d41c), [IWMT is agnostic about an artificial world modeler](record:b861e002-244a-45da-8747-3fd115d52639), and [intelligent zombies are conceivable but not feasible](record:79db9617-2c7a-4a07-99cd-f9a83a76ac0f).

Safron names and answers objections to the theories he combines. [The unfolding argument](record:3ec1578d-ad04-44d1-b70b-9eae03121b66) is met by [the infeasibility and fragility of unrolled networks](record:3ab6a065-f178-48b1-b024-796f34d1d752). [High integrated information in expander graphs](record:b119565f-5b71-4c94-84fc-fa93a19930cf) is met by [requiring coherent world modeling](record:d3f6e847-7b1c-454e-870c-e0b531899030). The sharpest stated test is that [perturbational complexity should be higher during workspace dynamics, and if it is not, IWMT is in trouble](record:27a84ea8-a4b5-4738-820f-b3457cd3db32).
