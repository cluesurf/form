# Conscious electromagnetic information (cemi) field theory

The cemi field theory starts from the binding problem: the parts of a scene, or the words of a line of poetry, are processed in separate places in the brain yet arrive as one experience. McFadden takes Landauer's dictum that information is physical at its word, so [integrated information must be physically integrated](record:31fb3902-c693-4868-82d1-8dc22612fd1c). Neurons, circuits and computers [integrate only in time](record:9f4186fa-fe97-4f6a-86b1-bf33b8c9f1c3): an output is correlated with many inputs, but no one physical state holds them all at once. [Only energy fields integrate information in space](record:e8666246-8535-4817-9f45-1d531be92c99), and a field can compute, running [an algorithm in space rather than in time](record:f3328fe9-a19d-4e14-8c3c-f65ab7c30bee).

The brain has such a field. Firing neurons make [its electromagnetic field, measured as EEG and MEG](record:71d81a2a-a60e-4466-9be4-5135c1fb7350), and the voltage-gated channels of neurons near threshold [respond to small field changes](record:d984b6a8-2362-43f8-9d35-280e4aa245b5). The theory's claim is that [the brain's field is the substrate of consciousness](record:2137b295-e763-4cb4-9b86-359debc6ab36): conscious thoughts are made of field energy rather than of matter, [a scientific dualism of matter and energy](record:63c2b7bb-f20a-4a14-a1ea-4fe2609ee7a8). Because a field algorithm only works when its nodes fire together, [conscious content should track synchrony rather than firing rate](record:9d28d0f9-f0fa-47a2-b36e-4dc1cf0d8654), and McFadden cites studies where it does. Because there is only one field, binding is given, [the conscious mind is serial](record:fefe15ff-71a8-478d-9a50-ee0cd26a8586), and [the conscious content is the strongest field perturbation able to move neurons](record:4bfcfdfc-c601-4bd6-96df-47d55fcda039), a measurable threshold where rival theories use arbitrary ones.

The field also acts. Its feedback on firing is [what is experienced as will](record:76da6b91-2406-4694-8fe4-6562312ebb97), fully causal and consistent with determinism. Natural selection, acting on helpful and harmful field effects, [split the brain into a field-sensitive conscious stream and a field-insulated nonconscious one](record:0cbbe8c9-e214-456e-b0d7-35f1d361b234). Experience itself is placed by a [double-aspect view of information](record:10b01953-7ce4-4975-a1c2-33f77ed9a9b9): [consciousness is what physically integrated information feels like from the inside](record:92355263-ca4b-4625-ab76-6aa9f01be09b). The theory [does not predict panpsychism](record:2920c405-6b30-4002-bf1e-7b0653c0c7c6), since only fields complex enough and able to act count, and it [forbids consciousness in computers that compute only through matter](record:39d63d23-382a-4268-853a-5df37e270bb9) while allowing it in [machines that compute through field interactions](record:bd4af72c-4ed8-4545-832b-cef3e4ee407c).

Critics hold that the brain's field is [a by-product with no role](record:9ba0fc97-2988-4aad-ab25-e6ccd7721e44), and older objections ask why [split-brain patients have two minds in one field](record:fb5d0c78-b98b-408c-8e9c-7621c573665d) and why [outside fields do not disturb thought](record:d1de00d6-6f8f-4c93-ba6d-84a5a84dcae8). McFadden answers that field effects fall off fast and need synchronous relays, and that outside fields are incoherent to a brain tuned to its own. The sharpest open test is [shaping an external field to reinforce or inhibit a response tied to an event-related potential](record:ee732343-b068-47c8-aad6-99ed24b9e393).
