# Biological Naturalism

Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted, unreviewed. Not written or endorsed by John Searle. To propose a correction, open an issue or pull request that changes the record and cites the source.

This is a [form.surf](https://form.surf) model repository for the biological naturalism of the philosopher John Searle: mental phenomena are caused by neurobiological processes in the brain and are themselves higher-level features of the brain, consciousness has a first-person ontology that cannot be reduced to third-person description, and instantiating a computer program is never by itself sufficient for understanding, which the Chinese room argument is built to show. Each claim is one JSON record in `proposition/`, with its kind (definitional, empirical, philosophical, normative), its epistemic status, and the source and page it was taken from. Most claims are philosophical. The claim that brain processes cause every conscious state is stated by Searle as empirical. Searle's own words come from his 1980 target article and from passages of his later works quoted in the critical papers by Daniel Dennett, Saulo de Freitas Araujo and Henry Stapp, and each such record cites the paper the quote was checked in. The replies Searle answered in 1980 and the later critiques are kept as claims with `voice: "critique"`.

Every record has `provenance.ai_written: true` and `reviewed: false` until a named person checks it against its sources.

Format: form.surf/1. Check with `form check .`.
