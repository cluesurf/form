# Biological Naturalism

Searle's starting point is a biological fact as he sees it: [intentionality in humans and animals is a product of causal features of the brain](record:9d070907-53ba-42a3-b1c9-ca1df520c13d), and [every conscious state is caused by neurobiological processes](record:341a8726-9d95-4086-a6bf-48f2fe2872db). He named the view in 1983, putting mental states [beside lactation, photosynthesis and digestion](record:89193d8a-5d5b-467c-a8c2-60bb4ff194a2). [Consciousness is a biological feature of certain organisms](record:25168224-e9ad-4da0-bff9-d4bd330d285d), a [causally emergent feature of systems of neurons](record:761773b4-3bd9-4ad7-af65-54fabeb226e7), related to its neural base [as liquidity is related to water molecules](record:a745b757-fa8a-4fc5-ae5c-c077c11328df).

What makes consciousness special is that [its ontology is first-person](record:97a7c164-cf06-4848-bfe1-11e26c5fd2f1). It is [causally reducible to neural processes but not ontologically reducible](record:91f8ac5a-ed99-4a09-a83e-ec4a0064bca0), and Searle says [the failure to reduce comes from the pattern of reduction chosen for pragmatic reasons](record:cdac3468-d67e-4930-b6ea-140d2b54156e), not from any mystery. He rejects both sides of the old debate: [materialism and dualism are both false](record:47e610e7-8257-406b-8307-ea5ea1f87f00), dualism because [no coherent account of reality can be given on its assumptions](record:7889d979-4571-46fe-8eb7-b989f17d3915). Subjective features are simply [moved to the side of the real world](record:d980c74f-cdc4-4a2f-a9b7-62a77e57bb22), and [conscious intentions cause bodily movement](record:fd240def-95fe-474d-9c5c-4cd808a6bda8).

The best known part is the argument against strong AI. [Instantiating a program is never by itself sufficient for intentionality](record:008a9697-49d8-48df-9678-7006dc8130be). In [the Chinese room](record:c267e36e-5f08-431c-9664-810f429b5430), a person follows English rules for Chinese symbols, answers as a native speaker would, and [still understands nothing](record:a5e87521-3e73-4f7c-ba7f-f0da691dff40). [Formal symbols have a syntax and no semantics](record:e8088d5b-77fe-4968-85be-87761b8e97a7), and [the information a computer processes is in the eyes of its programmers and users](record:6803d4f2-383e-4399-b03f-72216e7fc81c). [To simulate is not to duplicate](record:edae30b3-58bc-4603-b7b4-0166c45be34c). So [any mechanism that produces intentionality needs causal powers equal to the brain's](record:69d3937c-1b06-41cb-a964-29ebac8df43c), and [only machines with such powers can think](record:51bad06c-67c8-4249-b493-e3be0ff4861b), while [whether another chemistry could do it is an empirical question](record:782a09d7-c07f-4a7d-85eb-01ee82284576). Searle answers [the systems reply](record:ec71b181-8eb5-433b-a72e-17628adda7c5), [the robot reply](record:fd99a76b-7ea9-4c6f-9e8b-ff9093a41f67) and [the brain simulator reply](record:7a259f4d-fadc-4a8e-a29c-1a86668c70be) in the same paper.

The critics press on the middle of the view. Dennett finds that [Searle's own thought experiment leaves no way to investigate whether a silicon brain is conscious](record:f99cb6d4-20fd-4092-b245-1894f87981d8) and that [the evolutionary role he gives consciousness contradicts it](record:6642b34b-117f-40c9-b4ca-d7563f7d0e60). Araujo and others argue that [the view is a form of dualism after all](record:74d51240-1370-4b48-b7c9-9ec9048a66d8), that [mental causation then has two causes](record:347a8d04-0893-40db-924d-edf3b18ff5f6), and that [the neuron level is chosen arbitrarily](record:6dca86ac-3bd4-4f67-b47f-56846333051b). Stapp argues that [the causal premise is not shown by correlational evidence](record:6a7b73d5-e789-4732-93b6-e9193ca322cb) and that [the view slides back into epiphenomenalism](record:ddc64410-2c4e-4681-9f7d-cdf0855256b9).
