# Posner Quantum Cognition

**Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted (claude-opus-5-5), unreviewed. Not written or endorsed by Matthew P. A. Fisher.**
To propose a correction, open an issue or a pull request that changes the record in question, citing the source and locator that shows the error. A correction from Matthew P. A. Fisher or their collaborators is especially welcome.

Matthew Fisher's hypothesis that phosphorus nuclear spins, entangled by an enzyme and protected inside calcium phosphate Posner molecules, could carry quantum information in the brain and correlate the firing of distant neurons.

This is a form.surf model repository (format `form.surf/1`). It holds the framework's own claims, each as one JSON record in `proposition/`, with the evidence its originators cite and the evidence against it in `observation/`, the published criticism as propositions with `voice: "critique"`, and the relations between them in `link/`. Every record names its sources in `provenance.sources`, with a locator and, where it could be checked, a short verbatim quote. `paper/account.md` is the plain account.

## Labels

- `provenance.ai_written: true`: a model extracted the record from the cited source.
- `provenance.reviewed: false`: no person has yet read the record against its source.
- `voice: "critique"`: a published objection, not the framework's own claim.
- `standing`: null on every claim. The proposal is a hypothesis whose tests are under way, and its predictions carry no recorded outcome.
