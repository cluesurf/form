# Cosmopsychism

Panpsychism holds that consciousness is fundamental and ubiquitous. Its usual form is bottom-up: the smallest physical things have very simple experience, and the experience of humans and animals is built from theirs. [Cosmopsychism](record:d12e3498-ea05-4010-990f-0ffe925135e0) turns this upside down. On [priority monism](record:c0162938-d711-47e8-a899-e7a584681da3) the cosmos is the one basic concrete object and its parts derive from it, and on [priority cosmopsychism](record:bde43440-05f9-420a-831b-1dc0ba616d2b) [cosmic consciousness is the one basic consciousness](record:c99b5b2f-d877-4eb1-adea-90795d5ff806), from which every other consciousness is derived. The view is [top-down where panpsychism is bottom-up](record:d2f9b644-d976-45a1-8f87-47b9ed522d84).

Nagasawa and Wager argue that this keeps panpsychism's strength, that [it avoids strong emergence](record:7e8e398b-8a75-4001-befd-5563d68d6845), and loses two of its problems: [it needs no bottom level of reality](record:a4321d1b-e1cd-4c81-971d-21c1796a88cc), and [it does not face the combination problem](record:14b09c0c-433f-4523-b0da-61298ecce4e7), since a smooth experience can be a segment of a larger smooth whole though not a sum of dots. What it owes instead is [the derivation problem](record:f4490a33-17b6-4e3f-b959-d0ae3df93645), how ordinary consciousnesses come from the cosmic one, which they answer by analogy with the parts of one visual experience and with Schaffer's accounts of a heterogeneous cosmos. They stay [neutral on the nature of cosmic consciousness](record:0df4102d-1a1e-4626-8b8a-424cdd787abc). Nagasawa adds that [cosmopsychism is the parallel of pantheism](record:fbe550cf-ce47-4967-b0af-bb7aba818ee6), and that [pantheism entails it but not the reverse](record:402fb25e-fd9b-46fc-bf55-a8ac30e39165).

Goff reaches cosmopsychism through the grounding relation. Grounding is either truthmaking or [subsumption](record:5c649618-86be-46f8-a16c-2ea380034c60). [Micropsychism can only use truthmaking, which makes human minds shadows of discourse](record:667953ea-1df8-4587-852c-e48929deae01), while [cosmopsychism grounds by subsumption and keeps them real](record:e7b4a68a-6f81-4ad0-9656-913c0efd83f4). It needs [subjects that are aspects of other subjects](record:7e9c0f26-d236-4de0-9271-7c865583e4f9), which cannot be imagined but are coherent, and points to [consciousness+](record:af27e26c-efdd-4556-86d7-cf326a9e858e), a property with consciousness as one aspect. In his later [hybrid cosmopsychism](record:d77597ee-8c37-4e2d-ab00-557aff092ab5) local subjects strongly emerge but [inherit experience the universe already had](record:35e89801-00df-47ea-a85f-0f1cd8706e8c), under [laws of localization and thinning](record:23cbf73d-a815-4820-b2cd-6633814a97f3), so that [physics runs the same with or without them](record:14c4231e-892c-4d10-a3b1-82698e4df21b).

Shani and Keppler identify [cosmic consciousness with the zero-point field](record:bb924ada-42d2-4e89-a5e7-3ef18b551cd9) of stochastic electrodynamics. On their [filtering hypothesis](record:30228cc1-69b0-4582-b35d-9eb02aae8a91) a system in coherent resonance with the field draws its experience from it, so [no subject is cut from another](record:4f283d51-54d4-4e99-9285-9b925057564b). The field's experience is [implicit, not explicit](record:4e83114a-e857-416c-9f5f-9f56441ed08f), and its subject is [a pure, aperspectival subject](record:3ab3256d-7505-4be3-9a96-a05a10fad303) from which [perspectival subjects emerge](record:f8ee7540-455f-4f39-959b-cf855e86ae3b). [Structure modulates experience and does not generate it](record:dfa01a32-0b04-4c12-8575-be7f7baea788), and the brain's correlates of consciousness are [its modulation of the field](record:65a5dcb6-247d-4cca-986f-a919c5437472), which [predicts characteristic photon emission](record:33d6ac91-e92b-4da9-9349-4f95e657aa8a).

The main open question is [the de-combination problem](record:fa55f3e0-5613-4e37-95e9-a1fcabdb1eba): a conscious universe without conscious parts seems conceivable. Albahari presses that [the universe would hold contradictory thoughts](record:f55f7951-08e5-4cb9-9f4c-e9bb11c97b52), and others ask [what cosmic consciousness is](record:b2b5e4ff-45ba-4a14-9d37-9eb2a5bd5b77), why [there is no sign of it](record:c628244a-bb29-4aba-83ca-81dcb374b965), and whether [an explanatory gap remains](record:6408cfe8-b533-4512-8671-568b8f2ca329).
