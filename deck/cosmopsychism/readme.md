# Cosmopsychism

Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted, unreviewed. Not written or endorsed by Philip Goff, Yujin Nagasawa and Itay Shani. To propose a correction, open an issue or pull request that changes the record and cites the source.

This is a [form.surf](https://form.surf) model repository for cosmopsychism, the top-down form of panpsychism: the universe as a whole is conscious, its consciousness is ontologically prior to the consciousness of humans and animals, and ordinary subjects are derived from it. Its sources are Yujin Nagasawa and Khai Wager's priority cosmopsychism and Nagasawa's comparison of cosmopsychism with pantheism, Philip Goff's case that cosmopsychism grounds by subsumption where micropsychism must deflate human experience, his hybrid cosmopsychism, and Itay Shani and Joachim Keppler's version, which identifies cosmic consciousness with the zero-point field of stochastic electrodynamics. Each claim is one JSON record in `proposition/`, with its kind (definitional, empirical, philosophical), its epistemic status, and the source and page it was taken from. The theses of the view are marked hypothesized, what follows from them inferred, and the terms it introduces designed. Objections the sources raise or answer, chiefly the de-combination problem, are kept as claims with `voice: "critique"`.

Miri Albahari's analytic Buddhist and Advaitic version, perennial idealism, and her critique of cosmopsychism are not yet read from her own texts: her two papers had no copy open to a script, so her objections appear here only as the sources above report them.

Related folders: [`panpsychism`](../panpsychism/) holds the bottom-up view and the combination problem in full, with Galen Strawson's argument and Goff's phenomenal bonding. [`russellian-monism`](../russellian-monism/) holds the claim that physics describes only structure, which cosmopsychism shares. [`analytic-idealism`](../analytic-idealism/) holds Bernardo Kastrup's idealist cosmopsychism, on which the universe is one mind and living beings are its dissociated alters.

Every record has `provenance.ai_written: true` and `reviewed: false` until a named person checks it against its sources.

Format: form.surf/1. Check with `form check .`.
