# Review list for the seed proposal

Each claim below is written open, and the seed
proposal (`proposal/seed.json`) carries a set_standing change that
would make it integrated. Accept or decline each one. Ordered by
importance: how much of the shared model leans on it, then how strong
its evidence is. Every claim is AI-written and unreviewed.

1. [Posterior cortex holds activity for as long as a percept lasts](record:ea4beaba-c257-4a92-ac33-958f604d9014). Basis: Cogitate 2025 preregistered adversarial test, 25 of 657 posterior iEEG electrodes passing the IIT duration criterion.
2. [Stimuli people cannot report are still processed](record:5c24f0f9-c678-40cf-97f1-1d6cb0055159). Basis: fMRI of masked against visible words and high-density ERP masking series (Dehaene and colleagues, reported by Baars and Dehaene).
3. [Cortical responses to a pulse stay local in dreamless sleep](record:e0a7a845-367c-4fd4-a192-9d7fcba2ad17). Basis: TMS-EEG in NREM sleep against wakefulness, and the perturbational complexity index across sleep, three anesthetics and coma.
4. [A bioelectric state can store the body plan regeneration rebuilds](record:763d34ff-601b-451d-9800-df4d24c15fc3). Basis: two planarian experiments, one with voltage imaging and reversal, one secondhand (Levin summary of Emmons-Bell 2015).
5. [Effective information can peak at a macro scale](record:4fb02043-41c0-4811-b3f8-154a777a5bf8). Basis: a worked calculation (four noisy AND gates, 1.15 against 1.55 bits) under the stated convention.
