# Predictive Processing: the controlled hallucination and the beast machine

*Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted, unreviewed, not endorsed by Anil Seth. Each linked claim cites its source.*

Anil Seth argues that the brain does not read the world off its senses. It guesses. [Perceptual content is the brain's best guess](record:d06560be-270d-4cbc-97c7-5a2fb2967336) about the hidden causes of its sensory signals, built by top-down predictions and corrected by [prediction error](record:85c5678f-a366-45d1-b84b-b58f2f847abe), the mismatch between what was predicted and what arrived. Seth calls conscious perception a [controlled hallucination](record:7f7ae4dc-a773-4709-aab2-dd2de6e2ed8d): [what we experience is the content of those predictions](record:da202903-e7a6-4c14-abf5-54e2e6060ad6), held in check by the world. The brain can also cut prediction error [by acting so the prediction comes true](record:9813bc3f-cbd1-4204-b149-866e7cce5df3), which is [active inference](record:d12e5c4f-79e1-492a-89be-75b2161405d7). Seth treats this framework as [a tool for the science of consciousness rather than a finished theory of it](record:cc60224b-7209-4beb-9052-8c1602b3edc7), aimed at the [real problem](record:7bb9e382-e9d8-474b-9cd5-8b8c0f75e78e): explaining, predicting and controlling the properties of experience.

The same machinery turned inward gives emotion and the self. [Interoception](record:e6e5cf32-c55d-426c-ad22-f9bca11ef10a), the sense of the body from within, is also inference, and [emotions are the brain's predictions about the causes of bodily signals](record:bfea6bd6-b8c9-44e5-938d-61bbd85e353d). [Interoceptive predictions set the targets for autonomic reflexes](record:d8e4d0e5-03b3-4601-acc1-52cae18cde0b), so the brain regulates heart rate and blood pressure by predicting them. Seth and Manos Tsakiris hold that [selfhood rests on this control-oriented, or instrumental, inference](record:aa3d8ebd-d187-4b05-b746-0be0f45ab97c): its job is [keeping the body alive more than discovering facts](record:9f4551d6-03c3-4827-ab08-ccd9515fbe1b). That explains two oddities of the self. It [does not feel like an object](record:27e3822d-9b5e-43be-93f1-0d848eb75646), and it [feels stable even as it changes](record:c62fe600-c6f8-4959-b5cd-293eb9f55e3d). A heartbeat-synchronized virtual hand [feels more like one's own](record:54839570-d920-4c70-99fd-c47bdc8cf61d), the kind of evidence the account predicts.

From there Seth reverses Descartes. Descartes called animals beast machines without minds. Seth says [we are conscious selves because we are beast machines](record:e3f35112-cf88-494c-abd6-b0d3463d9642), living systems that must stay within viable bounds, and that [all perceptual content is grounded in that regulation](record:c7e91785-67f7-495d-bfe5-149060171d39). This supports a [continuity between life, mind and consciousness](record:3eea1110-e178-4ef4-9534-57dbef40fcd1) and a biological naturalism in which [consciousness depends on being alive](record:64ce15ca-292e-414c-a199-f6796aed5316), so that [conscious AI is unlikely on current trajectories](record:357c6638-59be-4013-8687-025f9d0ecd4c).

Seth names the account's open edges himself. [Direct evidence for interoceptive inference is scarce](record:da2d8f84-b2a3-47fb-a59c-a8a819af6817), and [how the machinery maps to feeling is open](record:b59f7b43-fcd7-4596-a1cd-fd0b476977e4). Critics say predictive processing [unifies by fiat](record:858fde75-7cb8-446d-abf9-dc127f817af2), faces [the dark room problem](record:56b6e530-3565-4159-8dc4-7ece3398a0af), and that [the heartbeat evidence for interoceptive selfhood is weak](record:af3ed9d1-0a74-4fde-9d0e-a8fb60c7aaf2).
