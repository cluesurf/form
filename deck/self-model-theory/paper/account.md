# Self-Model Theory of Subjectivity

The self-model theory begins from [constraints a representation must meet to be conscious](record:cdcc5452-ba66-4e4e-a311-1945b649f3c7). The simplest conscious state is [the presence of a world](record:21a2a6f2-1d2b-46a9-a3fd-a8a85b102836): a global model of reality, inside a window of presence, that the system cannot recognize as a model. That last condition is [phenomenal transparency](record:c0208e1c-f997-431c-bb5d-a9c3a189810b), defined as [the attentional unavailability of earlier processing stages](record:4af869ec-83ef-4955-a37e-14f1aeea12d9). It [comes in degrees](record:2ff5e11f-0e76-4113-b2df-0f83d628378d), with [opacity](record:d73804af-7c4d-492b-9651-669e92dfc1a5) at the other end, as in thought and lucid dreams, and it [locks the system into naive realism](record:4b28e51f-515f-47ea-b19d-08ebde0cf1c9).

Applied to the self, transparency does the decisive work. A [phenomenal self-model](record:5e6c6d41-3ab9-4c49-8d6a-62fd91d8cea4) is a conscious model of the system as a whole, and [a transparent self-model is enough for a phenomenal self](record:801589bb-4099-4078-836f-c095bb48fa9f). From this comes the central ontological claim, that [no such things as selves exist](record:cf9b402e-1981-4515-bac2-d174696bf16f): organisms exist and some have self-models, but [systems confuse themselves with the content of their self-model](record:8ab84e17-f7d6-4fc4-9f4b-72788acc5d0d). [Transparency blocks an infinite regress of self-modeling](record:34a1b9af-df8c-4787-ac33-3d5f6be173bb), which is why it evolved, and it makes a system [a naive realist about itself](record:22a65401-8207-4dba-9995-51c14a54db64). Even the [identity of a person across time is virtual](record:7982c683-d771-44cf-b93e-b642ee2a527e), and [there is no narrative self](record:8d1b5e5d-064e-40d4-bd43-e6c63ae56e9c).

The first-person perspective is the [phenomenal model of the intentionality relation](record:ea888beb-7ef6-458a-b775-73e51d60d352), a model of the system directed at an object. Iterating it yields [introspective and social intelligence](record:6d5822ae-b6cf-4dec-b2f2-db4fd358e337). Its simplest base is [minimal phenomenal selfhood](record:3a70ef4e-15a4-486e-bfd6-9c1c67277929), which bodiless dreams suggest [reduces to transparent self-location](record:95f41f52-021f-4dab-8ddd-bd30bbe058b0). When the self is modeled as a knower it becomes an [epistemic agent model](record:43450e03-2b8d-49de-b391-07cd2b9b9e74), which [collapses in mind wandering](record:215bbc5a-7698-4af2-8ff8-c480f6a0217b), so that [most conscious thought is subpersonal](record:52cb1bb0-d576-4d01-949e-3acd0f1051b8). [Mental action may be predictive control of effective connectivity](record:06258e2c-6d99-4842-a621-6be518429c45), and [the self as an ultimate originator may be an enculturated confabulation](record:3985ebd1-b1d3-4a08-8c04-64cf0fd32983).

Later work moves from the minimal self to [minimal phenomenal experience](record:f92de491-68e3-401c-8054-0005dc3c8b19), the "pure awareness" of meditation, which on the theory is [a Bayesian representation of tonic alertness](record:bd241ca0-083e-4f01-864d-8fdfbf6fb095) and [selfless](record:e7e104fb-de05-476f-8332-99eefa26b08a), so that [consciousness can exist without a self](record:103344c5-4c2b-4ccd-a595-79080126bb13).

Critics answer that [the theory assumes a classical notion of self](record:3c27aec7-7567-4b64-91a4-a39023b2cc70), that [a minimal self of first-personal givenness is real](record:f647cb27-7343-4df6-9d61-e7978168ff9b), that [an embodied self is real and not an illusion](record:611a5ce3-9ec8-49c3-90fc-91bc35bb3727), and that [consciousness always comes with self-consciousness](record:53100b16-0f60-4934-a905-db93a817a7bf).
