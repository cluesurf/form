# Self-Model Theory of Subjectivity

Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted, unreviewed. Not written or endorsed by Thomas Metzinger. To propose a correction, open an issue or pull request that changes the record and cites the source.

This is a [form.surf](https://form.surf) model repository for Thomas Metzinger's self-model theory of subjectivity, set out in Being No One (2003) and The Ego Tunnel (2009), summarized in his Psyche précis, and carried forward in papers on minimal phenomenal selfhood, mental autonomy, mental action and minimal phenomenal experience. Each claim is one JSON record in `proposition/`, with its kind, its epistemic status and the source and page it was taken from. The books are not open copies, so the claims are drawn from the précis and the open papers. Commentaries from the Psyche symposium on the book (Dan Zahavi, Shaun Gallagher, Josh Weisberg) and the active inference reading of transparency by Jakub Limanowski and Karl Friston are kept as claims with `voice: "critique"` where they object.

The theory shares two claims with the separate model `illusionism`: there is no self as a thing, only a self-model, and the feeling of direct contact with a self and a world comes from transparency, the system's blindness to its own representations as representations. It parts from illusionism on phenomenal experience itself. Metzinger treats phenomenal content as real, locally supervening on the brain and open to analysis by its constraints, and calls only the self an appearance. Illusionism denies that experiences have phenomenal properties at all.

Every record has `provenance.ai_written: true` and `reviewed: false` until a named person checks it against its sources.

Format: form.surf/1. Check with `form check .`.
