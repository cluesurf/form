# Dendritic Integration Theory

Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted, unreviewed. Not written or endorsed by Jaan Aru, Mototaka Suzuki and Matthew E. Larkum. To propose a correction, open an issue or pull request that changes the record and cites the source.

This is a [form.surf](https://form.surf) model repository for dendritic integration theory (DIT): the state and the contents of consciousness are coupled because layer 5 pyramidal neurons sit in both the cortico-cortical loop that carries content and the thalamo-cortical loop that sets state, and conscious processing depends on coupling between the apical and basal compartments of those neurons. Talis Bachmann co-authored the 2019 statement and a 2020 account of the theory, and is credited here as a co-author rather than an originator, following the review literature that cites the theory to Aru, Suzuki and Larkum.

The founding statement in Trends in Cognitive Sciences (2020) is not read here, so its claims are cited through the papers that state them: Aru, Suzuki, Rutiku, Larkum and Bachmann (2019), the arXiv preprint of Aru, Larkum and Shine (2023, published in Trends in Neurosciences, DOI 10.1016/j.tins.2023.09.009), and the review by Storm and colleagues (2024). The rodent experiments behind the theory, among them the anesthesia decoupling result and the perceptual threshold results, are in `observation/`, each pointing at the read paper that reports it. Each claim is one JSON record in `proposition/`, with its kind, its epistemic status, and the source and locator it was taken from. Objections from allied work on apical amplification (Marvan and colleagues 2021, Phillips and colleagues 2016 and 2018), from the 2024 review, and the open questions the authors list themselves are kept with `voice: "critique"`, and `link/` joins claims to each other and to evidence.

Every record has `provenance.ai_written: true` and `reviewed: false` until a named person checks it against its sources.

Format: form.surf/1. Check with `form check .`.
