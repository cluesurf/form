# Dendritic Integration Theory

Dendritic integration theory starts from a fact about experience: [the state of consciousness never comes apart from its contents](record:e6f5895a-ade3-4248-a4b9-51a337990521). Research on the [state of consciousness](record:fed1d196-c7d2-42be-9730-5932cb7d11c6) has looked at the thalamus, where [nonspecific thalamic nuclei set the state](record:4c670115-fba8-4b57-9111-7e4844c08243), while research on contents has looked at the cortex. The theory places the meeting point in one cell type. [Layer 5 pyramidal neurons have two sites of integration](record:7e8af1bb-bcf9-4a5a-a0a6-41f5efaee995), and [their basal dendrites take feedforward input while their apical dendrites take feedback and context](record:968e2718-81d6-404c-ba73-22d61915a71c). Because they sit in both loops, [layer 5 pyramidal neurons couple the state and the contents of consciousness](record:b6acb562-981e-4628-afa6-9656f0e92451).

The cellular step is coincidence: [input to both compartments at about the same time makes the neuron fire a burst](record:b4ea6a85-d64a-4dbe-95dc-3267e9c4354e), and [bursts reach the whole cortex through the nonspecific thalamus](record:013cb2ec-56be-4b4b-9fb7-095146869ae8). During conscious states [the two compartments are coupled](record:44275afa-efe4-45b8-b986-cef4f56df909), and [a junction between them works as a gate on cortical output](record:2dcaa1e8-a7f8-46e9-95d6-48183879a059). [Higher-order thalamus and neuromodulators control that gate](record:fc97ca27-dd84-4315-8a9e-d0c7770e6d94). The theory's sharpest claim follows: [cortical processing that does not reach layer 5 neurons and the thalamus is not conscious](record:8b9ced59-dab1-47f9-a3d3-1a931d78ff0b).

The evidence is mostly from rodents in Larkum's laboratory. [Anesthesia decouples the apical from the basal compartment](record:4fb328f4-721a-4f9b-ab89-42eb37e7f101), and [suppressing apical activity or its thalamic targets suppresses detection of threshold stimuli](record:019560d9-3312-4c19-a112-4c04795f09c2). [Calcium spikes in these dendrites can be seen in surface recordings](record:4750a4cd-bb52-4a1f-9215-e9fafd4d674b), which gives the theory an EEG marker. The same view [explains backward masking as a race through the thalamic loop](record:92a620a6-fd5e-400f-930b-82e0bb85b36d) and [ties the time grain of experience to loop propagation](record:850eec99-f7c2-4bf1-872b-3f32b2a65f59).

Applied to machines, the authors argue that [large language models lack the features the theory needs](record:b016ce88-7dbb-4acd-9a64-064927e58623) and [are not conscious](record:f46c9fea-bba3-4165-9e67-9eb20ad8f834), while leaving open that [consciousness may be bound to the organization of living systems](record:068f840e-bdd4-4528-9ed6-ad3ff4275df6). Allied researchers on apical amplification hold that [the apical and somatic contents need no match](record:55b46440-fc70-4529-a537-b458f6346d37), that [amplification is not integration](record:e5323019-332c-48d4-905a-6da608444b1a), and that [DIT leaves out adrenergic arousal](record:09c41c6b-7b41-4f1c-bc9e-6f5fe7e04e87). Others note that [dreams occur with apical amplification at a minimum](record:fd109f97-905d-4f4b-95a5-5714bdc70459) and that [the theory's mechanism appears late in development and evolution](record:d773240a-1ceb-415f-b60a-4beac91f7d9e). The authors list their own open questions, starting with [how little is known about human layer 5 neurons](record:b0aa8f96-8ef7-4174-b049-41dc69099a0a).
