# Constitutive Geometric Projection

Constitutive Geometric Projection starts from one claim: [what exists is constitutive activity](record:f5065c95-590c-4a3f-b13a-a4d13482a391), the activity that makes entities entities. An entity is not a substance bearing properties, an instance of a Form, or a pattern in flux. It is [constituted, not given](record:dd83771b-6bdd-4ddd-84e0-fe649c5e206b), and [identity emerges](record:05c0fc25-2c5a-4fac-a9d2-59faa5d26321) when the activity satisfies [three projections](record:428fb4e0-e6b2-4194-a18d-cac4d53d8b12): a genealogical one (this causal history), a structural one (one thing across time) and a functional one (the activity is what the entity is being). The projections are not a checklist. They are [three readings of one irreducible structure](record:4e1054e2-1341-467d-9630-e85686f32dc8), so there is no partial entity. The structure is [geometric rather than propositional](record:f17de01c-6924-4f36-823a-b0d080fdd0df), in an abstract dimensional sense rather than a spatial one, and it is [known through projection](record:018012ed-805b-4d0f-8467-f0050f84ccc3), which the position takes to be realist.

The evidence the program offers is its own apparatus, [operators and interrogative witnesses](record:e585e065-e69a-4ab8-ac11-7ca6b619cddd) with [closure and completeness proofs kept in a private archive](record:5721ac0a-9a67-486f-9193-99b1b68ca826), and [operator patterns recovered from sperm whale codas](record:4bfc8d91-70d5-4948-862a-711743cca13e), with public replication pending. The program states that [the apparatus is the evidence](record:ac34623f-5d25-433e-bf07-e3e145dc1df9).

Applied to mind, the projections become [the three conditions of consciousness](record:3b4eae01-5a6a-4f15-8892-5eb6b4ec09f5): genealogical continuity, structural invariance and functional consequence, all at once. [Consciousness is what being the entity is](record:047aa9aa-5e60-4846-a40b-1e81e3d90ac7), so [sleep, anesthesia and coma are modes](record:047a00e1-20d8-47f5-97cb-2a16a22d9380) of one continuing activity, and [being unconscious means not existing](record:a8129ef5-5676-469a-987f-4c16f0d050a0). On these conditions [a large language model is not conscious](record:55dbade2-22b0-4dfa-83a2-78a8f2649814), [a river is not](record:41583567-57ae-4bfd-bcdf-92f33791e88e), and [every human is a person](record:710eab38-1e12-4848-953e-dca36325c148), since [behavior cannot detect constitution](record:5dd44bf8-69ad-4cea-a49f-95b16327075a).

The hard problem, on this view, is [an artifact of an inquiry](record:82bcd45e-c07b-4776-960b-251e46d7f6fb) that kept the structural and functional projections and [displaced the genealogical one](record:9fc28cda-50fe-45d9-a8db-0c175e0cc909). The same gap is said to appear [at the proof boundary of open problems in mathematics](record:88ef34a6-2caa-4ad8-aa17-41d729ac7155) and [in the observer vocabulary of physics](record:da3f7642-61e3-4ef0-8629-5a53337c2581). Integrated information theory is [diagnosed as bridge-debted](record:6fe76ee5-770e-46f8-8987-e9acaad76f68): it demands a bridge between two domains whose separation it never witnesses, and the replacement offered is [admissibility under a witness regime](record:0e6b6f33-ccd0-49f2-bd33-82722b06e361). Commenters on the posts object that [the proofs are withheld](record:7d579374-1b3f-4ef8-b779-2bffbbacdd5a), that [the framework makes no numerical predictions](record:0742fad8-24d7-484f-be5c-54bc950c040f), and that [physics already defines measurement without observers](record:3f8ab9ad-14e4-47bd-be85-c037ffa3e405).
