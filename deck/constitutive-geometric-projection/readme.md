# Constitutive Geometric Projection

Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted, unreviewed. Not written or endorsed by Devon Generally. To propose a correction, open an issue or pull request that changes the record and cites the source.

This is a [form.surf](https://form.surf) model repository for Constitutive Geometric Projection (CGP), the position the MetaCortex Dynamics research program names as its metaphysics, published on its Substack from March 2026 and deposited on Zenodo. The position holds that constitutive activity is what exists, that an entity exists if and only if that activity satisfies three projections of one geometric structure at once (genealogical, structural, functional), and that consciousness is not a property an entity has but what being the entity is. The program pairs the position with an apparatus of operators and interrogative witnesses whose closure and completeness proofs, by its own account, sit in a private archive, with public replication pending.

Each claim is one JSON record in `proposition/`, with its kind, its epistemic status and the post and section it was taken from. The position's metaphysical claims are philosophical. Its claims about whale codas, stellar fusion and open problems in mathematics are empirical or mathematical and are all `hypothesized`, since no public source reports their replication. Objections from the posts' public comment threads are kept as claims with `voice: "critique"`, and `link/` joins claims to each other and to the questionnaire.

Every record has `provenance.ai_written: true` and `reviewed: false` until a named person checks it against its sources.

Format: form.surf/1. Check with `form check .`.
