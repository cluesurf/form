# Quantum observers

**Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted (claude-opus-5-5), unreviewed. Not written or endorsed by Chris Fields or his collaborators.**
To propose a correction, open an issue or a pull request that changes the record in question, citing the source and locator that shows the error. A correction from Chris Fields or his collaborators or their collaborators is especially welcome.

Chris Fields's account of observers as quantum systems that exchange finite classical information across holographic screens, give it meaning through quantum reference frames, and so obey a quantum free energy principle.

This is a form.surf model repository (format `form.surf/1`). It holds the framework's own claims, each as one JSON record in `proposition/`, with the evidence its originators cite and the evidence against it in `observation/`, the published criticism as propositions with `voice: "critique"`, and the relations between them in `link/`. Every record names its sources in `provenance.sources`, with a locator and, where it could be checked, a short verbatim quote. `paper/account.md` is the plain account.

## Labels

- `provenance.ai_written: true`: a model extracted the record from the cited source.
- `provenance.reviewed: false`: no person has yet read the record against its source.
- `voice: "critique"`: a published objection, not the framework's own claim.
- `standing`: null throughout. The framework's biological predictions are recorded with `prediction.outcome` null, since no published result has yet resolved them.
