# Invariant Set Theory, the plain account

Tim Palmer, a climate physicist, starts from the geometry of chaos. Edward Lorenz's weather model does not wander through all of its possible states: its trajectories settle onto a fractal invariant set, a sparse, infinitely detailed shape of zero volume. Palmer's postulate is that the whole universe is like this ([the postulate](record:594e5057-8be3-4455-a819-6e1dbf2d1d34)). It is a deterministic system whose states lie exactly on a measure-zero fractal set $I_U$ in its state space, and the geometry of that set, not differential equations in spacetime, is the most basic statement of the laws of physics ([geometry first](record:6433a8d1-70a8-4753-8e04-0c9601138767)). A state that is easy to imagine but lies in one of the set's gaps is simply not physical.

That one move does most of the work. Counterfactual experiments, the measurement that could have been made but was not, typically lie off the set, so quantum complementarity follows ([counterfactuals](record:7c8c80f9-bebc-4c30-92c6-e742f18de37a)). In Bell experiments the theory violates Statistical Independence, the assumption that hidden variables are independent of measurement settings, so it is superdeterministic in that formal sense, yet locally causal and, Palmer argues, not conspiratorial or fine tuned, because distances on the set are measured by a $p$-adic metric under which the problematic perturbations are large ([Bell](record:fadd177c-a18b-423a-ae0c-9a8e4b9332e0)). Quantum states become finite ensembles over a discretized Hilbert space whose squared amplitudes and phases are rational, and ordinary quantum mechanics is the singular limit of that finite theory as the prime $p$ goes to infinity ([singular limit](record:aed9422a-c4c3-4468-984c-12cc7539cbf8)). The measurement problem is solved trivially, since there is no continuous wavefunction to collapse.

Gravity enters as the large-scale unevenness of the invariant set, while quantum processes are its fine-scale fractal structure. There is no graviton and no reason to quantize gravity ([no graviton](record:27205063-3aa7-4f7a-84e2-ca670ce7040c)). A modified Einstein equation lets spacetime curvature feel matter on neighboring trajectories, which would look like dark matter ([dark matter](record:6e0b1c31-cba8-491a-9cc7-7e6aefb54566)). The set is non-computable, following Roger Penrose, and Palmer extends this to the mind. Creativity comes from the brain's mix of noisy, energy-starved neurons and deterministic ones, and consciousness is an awareness of nearby counterfactual worlds that arises from quantum processes in neurons used for energy efficiency ([consciousness](record:09a4c649-3c16-4449-a2f2-ab1c0746df41)). Quantum physics feels strange because human intuition takes every nearby counterfactual world to be possible.

The theory is meant to be tested. With Sabine Hossenfelder, Palmer proposes measuring states prepared as nearly identically as possible, with small, cold detectors in quick succession, to look for time correlations the Born rule forbids ([test](record:43e45f59-77b6-4b07-a577-818dd4f571ab)). The finite length of the bit strings behind Hilbert states also implies that only a limited number of qubits can be entangled and still show quantum statistics. Indrajit Sen has built a hidden-variable model of the theory and argues it is nonlocal and conspiratorial, and Palmer and colleagues reply that the model is not invariant set theory.
