# basal-cognition

**Community-written from public sources. AI-extracted (claude-opus-5-5) and unreviewed. Not written or endorsed by Michael Levin or his collaborators.** To propose a correction, open an issue or a pull request that changes the record and cites the source passage, page or section included. Every record lists its sources in `provenance.sources`.

A form.surf model repository for Michael Levin's basal cognition program: cognition as a continuum present in cells and tissues, selves defined by the reach of their goals (the cognitive light cone), bioelectric networks that bind cells into larger selves, and the experimental record of planaria, tadpoles and xenobots.

- `paper/account.md`: the account in plain words, linked to its claims
- `proposition/`: the claims, one per file, with `voice: "critique"` for published criticism
- `observation/`: the experiments the program cites
- `link/`: dependencies, support, objections and questionnaire answers
- `source/`: the papers every record cites

Format: form.surf/1. Check with `form check .`. License CC-BY-4.0 for these records. The quoted passages belong to their authors.
