# Basal Cognition: minds at every scale

*Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted, unreviewed, not endorsed by Michael Levin. Each linked claim cites its source.*

Michael Levin argues that cognition did not start with brains. [Basal cognition](record:d97bfdbc-ab5c-4844-8f3e-738792f24f3f) studies the ways any living system, a bacterium, a cell or a tissue, senses, remembers, values and acts on its world. On his view [cognition is a continuum with no bright line](record:25da7545-7e6d-4674-afb7-e7e2191e1ec8) between real minds and mere physics, and [how much agency a system has is settled by experiment](record:9ff0f052-ee39-4913-948e-fa529b259194), not by definition: by which kind of intervention, from rewiring parts to offering rewards, best predicts and controls it, its [persuadability](record:d7ead889-69d2-4ac3-ba19-b5e082cdb6a6). [Intelligence is competent navigation](record:3ddf4084-fdd1-4b1b-8c51-a272ec5478e3) of any space toward better regions, including the space of possible anatomies, and [our estimates of it depend on us as observers](record:33fb6a41-d6f1-4da7-b1c9-148dd60c1fa2).

A [self is defined by the goals it can pursue](record:38bd1a2f-f0af-40cf-8d1a-54774464af00). The reach of those goals in space and time is its [cognitive light cone](record:e6252fbd-58ab-4f65-b36e-bf5df2c552d4): a tick's is small, a person's large. [Larger goals grow from the homeostatic drive to reduce stress](record:9d71d7ec-cb23-46f0-b7d0-d47fdc3841c4), and [bioelectric networks give the path](record:e5977b37-c6b1-4486-aee0-580e0e2ba889) from single-cell regulation to memory and prediction. [Gap junctions bind cells into larger selves](record:0ea669e5-1c9a-4e83-81fc-9a77105cd1a5) by letting signals pass between their interiors, so [selves are nested](record:28da1935-bf2a-4b7c-91a5-fc1912fa23ac) and [not fixed](record:8df47f5b-142c-47cf-aaf2-e2d4f0ce8260). When a cell disconnects, the self shrinks: [cancer is a shrinking of the self](record:b160b9a8-729d-437f-af7c-fcc11994e25f).

The experimental record is where the view earns its keep. [Morphogenesis is the behavior of a collective intelligence](record:89ae12d1-c509-42fd-a88b-e4426a6a1d99) working toward a [target morphology](record:a6f268a5-4e82-43c0-97e7-c0970b5abc14) stored as [bioelectric pattern memory](record:35e99e05-7055-48cd-8e06-7c142c911cad). A brief bioelectric perturbation makes planaria that [keep regenerating two heads](record:e1ead7a4-e2f6-43cf-b10e-765b0dd5f61e) with an unchanged genome. Changing membrane voltage [induces eyes outside the head](record:8a81b314-66c0-49d6-bdb8-2fb33ec51e27). Tadpoles with [scrambled faces correct them](record:2cdadae2-049f-4998-88dd-00866414f182). Frog cells assembled to designed shapes form [new living machines](record:c43b9d2e-f55b-4e99-96c7-53dc2dd0e5da) that can [replicate by gathering loose cells](record:14d0797f-1804-467e-99f0-f4df5a883c36). Levin takes these to show that [biomedicine can steer goals instead of micromanaging molecules](record:ff187b20-57a0-4f44-a7a2-679ca6ac78fb).

Levin extends the continuum, provisionally, to experience: [consciousness comes in degrees and kinds](record:76f6703f-2916-4878-98ee-753d0bb9cad5), and [systems acting in morphogenetic spaces may be minimally conscious](record:eefdca41-c999-4c83-bf3c-8c95eaabcfb3). He also notes that [the bioelectric hypotheses stand without these broader claims](record:df048aa6-73e2-4131-82f1-c5834376ca7b). Critics argue that [non-neural learning may not be cognition](record:7b1d50d3-cfb3-4dcc-abb8-70e32c8f64f7), that [cognition needs representations cells lack](record:fe3a1a8b-704f-4329-98cf-d42798353e8c), and that [assumed continuity ignores convergent evolution](record:32130890-89f9-434b-a24b-a0a3a5ef95ea).
