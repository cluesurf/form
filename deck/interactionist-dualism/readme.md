# Interactionist Dualism

Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted, unreviewed. Not written or endorsed by Karl R. Popper, John C. Eccles and Richard Swinburne. To propose a correction, open an issue or pull request that changes the record and cites the source.

This is a [form.surf](https://form.surf) model repository for interactionist dualism: the view that mind and body, or mental and physical events, are distinct and causally influence each other. Its twentieth-century statement is Karl R. Popper and John C. Eccles's The Self and Its Brain (1977). Eccles then proposed where the interaction happens: mental units, psychons, each linked to a dendron, a bundle of apical dendrites in the cortex, acting through a quantum probability at the synapse that the Beck-Eccles model of 1992 made specific. Richard Swinburne gives the modern philosophical case for the soul as the essential part of a person. Eccles's papers have no open full text that a script may fetch, so his claims are quoted from their published abstracts, and the 1977 book is recorded without quotes. Each claim is one JSON record in `proposition/`, with its kind, its epistemic status, and the source and page it was taken from. The objections from energy conservation, causal closure and the gap between kinds are kept as claims with `voice: "critique"`, beside the replies.

Related folders: [epiphenomenalism](../epiphenomenalism/) is the dualism that gives up mental causation, and records the natural selection argument Popper and Eccles pressed against it. [stapp-quantum-mind](../stapp-quantum-mind/) holds another quantum account of mind acting on brain. [orch-or](../orch-or/) also places a quantum process at the base of consciousness. [biological-naturalism](../biological-naturalism/) holds Searle's rejection of both dualism and materialism.

Every record has `provenance.ai_written: true` and `reviewed: false` until a named person checks it against its sources.

Format: form.surf/1. Check with `form check .`.
