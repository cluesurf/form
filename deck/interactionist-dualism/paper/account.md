# Interactionist Dualism

[Interactionism](record:2422f823-776d-41bf-b984-29fae488a1c1) is the view that mind and body causally influence each other, [the common-sense belief](record:48bb93b4-4b61-433b-acbc-e849097711de), and [substance dualism](record:e01c1aba-2348-44e1-8cc2-f46afa50f5bf) holds that minds and bodies are distinct kinds of substance. Popper and Eccles called the mental world [World 2](record:417e4638-27b3-49e4-869e-e5cd95c1b376), and argued that consciousness, [being a product of natural selection](record:85c2ae04-5977-4269-9956-7c8820d10231), must act.

Eccles proposed where. The cortex is built of [dendrons](record:eb1fb5a6-d3a3-4ecd-b9c6-1957d42a3de7), bundles of about a hundred apical dendrites, and the mental world is [microgranular, made of psychons](record:3ecbc4f4-74b6-4ab2-ac8f-e9591a0566ce), each linked to one dendron, so the mind-brain problem [reduces to dendron-psychon interaction](record:74335e2f-0c49-46ce-b870-c933198c319c), [running both ways](record:75ca8311-0fb3-4112-93ae-2f95f4f2d170). With Friedrich Beck he modeled [exocytosis as quantum tunneling](record:95269e8e-d5a7-4ec8-b8f2-314364d1a69c) with a probability well below one, so that [an intention acts by raising that probability](record:1a3e40da-b949-4988-8c75-13c937879e0f), which Eccles held [violates no conservation law](record:b83da8b7-fdc3-4bec-bc40-264c4ae42647). Psychons [came to exist with the mammalian neocortex](record:0ee4c919-993b-44ca-bb8f-86232f7d38ca), which gave mammals [an advantage over reptiles](record:bf558733-39a6-4bae-ac9c-2190b22664bc).

Swinburne argues that [mental events are distinct from brain events](record:6cb04be5-6e32-4138-b06e-247af3386e08), that [what happens to every physical part leaves open what happens to the person](record:2602c52c-bc09-49af-a752-834878ced4c2), and so [the soul is the essential part of a person](record:4a5e34f9-b781-46dd-a95b-4289df85454d), with [the body a contingent part](record:e9aac385-ef6d-4820-85dc-bed4ed39c029).

The objections are that [radically different kinds cannot interact](record:e0d214f8-f13e-44c3-b375-5aac3ab6f66d), that [interaction would break energy conservation](record:b67b5a6f-831d-4c12-a5d5-98810eba3a32), and that [the physical world is causally closed](record:e7e323c8-f92d-4e19-a974-d8fcccdeb64c). Defenders reply that [causation need not be impact](record:d41f3830-7ac4-4db4-a931-48a61fa076d9), that [conservation holds only for closed systems](record:7f446ea3-06fd-4538-89aa-917fd56ca1b5), that [conservation is not universal in physics](record:5e1bc10c-eb8b-415f-9e0d-c88a7bc06c24), and that [quantum indeterminacy leaves room](record:48f66e39-cd5b-427d-bdc1-0d84262e764b), while Cucu and Pitts argue that [the conservation-preserving proposals fail](record:d87538f6-c9ef-4309-ab23-d8a2a70eb431) and that [the question is empirical](record:6f00ceb6-393c-4d14-b72c-0a1e61fc56b2).
