Orch OR joins two arguments. The first is Roger Penrose's, from The Emperor's New Mind and Shadows of the Mind: by Gödel's theorem, [human understanding cannot be the output of any computation](record:9a3b1f40-da9e-4053-8439-72331c05d855). If the brain does something no algorithm does, it must use physics we do not yet have, and Penrose places that gap in [the measurement problem of quantum theory](record:3b7b1f70-7173-4c7b-a64c-65b8705402f5). His answer is [objective reduction](record:da87023c-1ec0-4e97-bf1d-240555d98823): a superposition of two mass distributions is a [superposition of two slightly different spacetime geometries](record:3be86ca9-e2bd-4465-833c-6118e9e9f932), which is unstable and [collapses on its own after about $\hbar / E_G$](record:f3e07445-9afa-4d95-bdce-df173801be7b), where $E_G$ is the [gravitational self-energy of the difference between the two](record:9946753e-0606-4650-b517-8385c32cd607). Each such collapse is [a moment of proto-conscious experience](record:f03eaec7-a5cd-4015-8f83-d3012021ec34), not caused by an observer.

The second argument is Stuart Hameroff's biology. Inside neurons, microtubules, the hollow protein cylinders of the cell skeleton, hold enough tubulin to [process vastly more than neurons firing](record:4fe12b70-fc05-419c-86ac-a6b0cf244018). Hameroff and Penrose propose that [coherent quantum states in collections of microtubules](record:f992f9e0-4750-4368-93d8-934fbf1a78f2), organized by the cell and [isolated long enough to reach the collapse threshold](record:fc7311b8-335b-4f66-8b50-2938a50cc94a), end in orchestrated reductions that are [moments of conscious awareness](record:9abc97e6-7a2f-4c55-9d2d-199f914c8d45), perhaps [40 times a second](record:014754d3-ddd6-483c-a8e4-db6d3346b26e). Anesthetics, which erase consciousness while sparing much brain activity, [should act through microtubules](record:f2a382ca-d376-4d98-aa2e-c216e86abcff). They conclude that [consciousness is intrinsic to the universe](record:9ff2ed23-d569-4a6c-a695-4dcfb642adaf).

The record is mixed. Rats given a microtubule stabilizer [took longer to lose consciousness under isoflurane](record:6efd0152-7cea-4964-b856-56d7d9d55f7a), and anesthetics [reduce energy migration in microtubules](record:01e165f7-f4fc-4f86-a769-7cc2518c8c95). An underground test at Gran Sasso [ruled out the simplest, parameter-free Diósi-Penrose model](record:e5557e6f-f6d2-4d74-a489-9596ba14c7eb), which leaves versions with a free size parameter open. Max Tegmark's calculation that [decoherence is far too fast in the brain](record:ae11fdf9-7319-4218-aef1-ba8f8ce65088) remains contested, with Hameroff and colleagues [recalculating much longer times](record:11253bce-6a65-49a6-828e-75040db5312a). Critics argue that the 2014 revision [specifies no qubit](record:d8f1a556-b258-4b77-bba8-0343be85bb10), and Solomon Feferman finds [the Gödelian argument marred by errors](record:7f8bf988-3713-4e96-9aa2-376dbed8f107).
