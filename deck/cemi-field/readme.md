# Conscious Electromagnetic Information (cemi) Field Theory

Community-written from public sources, AI-extracted, unreviewed. Not written or endorsed by Johnjoe McFadden. To propose a correction, open an issue or pull request that changes the record and cites the source.

This is a [form.surf](https://form.surf) model repository for the conscious electromagnetic information (cemi) field theory of Johnjoe McFadden: consciousness is the information encoded in the brain's endogenous electromagnetic field, the one physical structure in the brain that integrates information in space at a single moment. Each claim is one JSON record in `proposition/`, with its kind (definitional, empirical, philosophical), its epistemic status, and the source and page it was taken from. The theory's predictions and its account of the conscious and nonconscious streams are empirical. Its identity of the field with experience, and its double-aspect view of information, are philosophical. Findings McFadden cites from other laboratories are in `observation/`, each pointing at the paper that reports it, and `link/` joins claims to each other and to evidence. Published objections, including those McFadden answers, are kept as claims with `voice: "critique"`.

Every record has `provenance.ai_written: true` and `reviewed: false` until a named person checks it against its sources.

Format: form.surf/1. Check with `form check .`.
