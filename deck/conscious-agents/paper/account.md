# The account

This repository states, from public sources, the program of the cognitive scientist Donald Hoffman and his collaborators Chetan Prakash, Manish Singh, Chris Fields and Robert Prentner. It is community-written and AI-extracted, not written or endorsed by them.

The program starts from evolution. Our senses were shaped by natural selection, so the question is whether selection rewards seeing the world as it is. Hoffman and his colleagues model perception as a strategy competing in evolutionary games, and their answer is no: strategies tuned to fitness payoffs routinely beat strategies tuned to the truth ([the dominance result](record:a7907ffd-c41a-4ec6-a71b-961967759777)), and they prove a [Fitness-Beats-Truth theorem](record:07146716-c6ad-45f9-aa53-a4554416f42a) and a [result about structure](record:f3fc1a1e-ac4f-44e3-bf28-9b8dee6af710) that make the point mathematically. Their conclusion is the [interface theory of perception](record:15eb5784-eda9-416f-b2d6-865b0d94a29c): what we see is a species-specific user interface, like a computer desktop. [Space-time is the desktop and physical objects are its icons](record:4f1fce69-f254-4fe8-8357-3f0ac9445794). The icons are useful because they hide the machinery behind them.

The interface theory says what perception is not. [Conscious realism](record:5e3e8519-290a-44e2-afce-36a4247ecbf7) says what lies behind the interface: a world made entirely of conscious agents. A [conscious agent](record:88b5b2d2-2646-45d3-83d8-2232c7e474bf) is a precise mathematical object, a space of possible experiences together with Markov kernels for perceiving, deciding and acting. Agents [join into larger agents](record:3d701485-fb7c-476d-89f0-7bafdc660393), networks of agents [can compute anything a Turing machine can](record:6ffd4118-8bc7-4bd8-bfdb-4f88906384b2), and on this view [consciousness creates brain activity](record:fccaa18c-26ed-48d8-94a9-67b02d34e5f9) rather than the reverse. The authors state that the hypothesis is precise and [might be precisely wrong](record:c905c51d-da88-458a-b118-3410144c5e8b).

The recent work, Fusions of Consciousness, tries to recover physics from the agents. Agent dynamics live on a [Markov polytope](record:1305fd0a-16b2-4d5b-a3c6-c42316067d7d), which the authors connect through [decorated permutations](record:e233ace7-82e9-4e56-9eb5-e8d19bc6298d) to the positive geometries physicists use for particle scattering. The conjecture is that [a particle is a projection of a communicating class of agents](record:2ea675b2-c7ed-4034-9af4-22403f8f24d4), and that [entropic time is an artifact of that projection](record:46fc3e40-3d62-4364-9ff2-e718c95dd797). How mass, momentum and spin come out of it is [still open](record:30bf344e-36f3-4b87-8c85-80997c23ae1b). Critics dispute the evolutionary argument ([flexible goals favor veridical perception](record:e8231d5b-a0b5-4bab-8d89-2780f8781d1c), [the debunking argument defeats itself](record:cdb83291-096a-41be-8f7a-75e14edc24dd)) and the step from a formalism to an ontology ([models show behavior, not nature](record:558d41a7-8e79-497f-9850-145d8b7feca6)).
